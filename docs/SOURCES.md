# Sources

What the app reads from. Update as we discover new ones or retire dead links.

## Primary — meeting data

| Source | URL | Used for |
|--------|-----|----------|
| BoardDocs (Boone County) | https://go.boarddocs.com/il/boone/Board.nsf/Public | Agendas, minutes, packets for Board and most sub-bodies. Structured. |
| County Board Minutes archive | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/county_board/county_board_minutes/cb_minutes.php | Backup if BoardDocs is missing something. |
| YouTube — Boone County Government | https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJd8c3sZs98mx9vznx9nsOg/videos | Meeting video and captions. |

## Primary — structural info (for general summary)

| Source | URL | Used for |
|--------|-----|----------|
| County Board page | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/county_board/index.php | Members, districts, COTW structure. |
| County Board Duties | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/county_board/county_board_duties.php | What the Board is responsible for. |
| Departments index | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/departments/index.php | Department directory. |
| Clerk & Recorder | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/departments/clerk___recorder/index.php | Clerk's role and current officeholder. |
| Planning Dept. — boards & commissions | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/departments/planning_department/commissions_boards_and_committees.php | Roster for Planning Commission, ZBA, Ag Conservation. |
| Board of Health | https://www.boonecountyil.gov/government/departments/health_department/board_of_health.php | Health Board meeting info. |

## Secondary — one-time grounding

Used only when rebuilding `general_summary.md`. Not pulled monthly.

- Wikipedia — Boone County, IL
- Ballotpedia — Boone County, IL elected officials
- Illinois State Archives (IRAD) — https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/archives/irad/boone.html
- 55 ILCS 5 (Illinois Counties Code) — statutory powers of county boards

## Additional bodies (separate from BoardDocs)

## Considered and excluded (for now)

- **Local newspapers** (Boone County Journal, Rockford Register Star): useful color but data lineage gets muddy. Manual sanity check only.
- **Municipal sites** (Belvidere, Poplar Grove): out of scope.
