## Overview

Boone County is located in northern Illinois, bordering Wisconsin to the north, with McHenry County to the east, DeKalb County to the south, and Winnebago County to the west. The county seat is Belvidere. As of the 2020 Census, Boone County had a population of 53,448, with an estimated 53,568 residents as of 2025. The county covers 282 square miles (280.7 square miles of land, 1.3 square miles of water). Boone County was formed on March 3, 1836, from Winnebago County and is named after Kentucky frontiersman Daniel Boone.

Boone County government operates under the Committee of the Whole structure. The County Board serves as the legislative body responsible for policy decisions, including adopting the annual budget and appropriation ordinances, establishing tax policy, appointing committees and commissions, overseeing county departments, and providing county buildings and services. Board members are elected to two- or four-year terms from three districts, with four members from each district. The Board Chair and Vice Chair are elected by board members, and the Chair has authority to appoint committees and fill committee vacancies. All County Board meetings and standing committees are open to the public except for Closed Session.

## The County Board

- **Composition:** 12 elected members; 4 members from each of 3 districts
- **Leadership:** Chair and Vice Chair elected by board members
- **Meeting location:** County Board Room, 1212 Logan Avenue, Belvidere, IL, 6:30 P.M.
- **Public access:** Meetings available on-site and via YouTube Live Stream; public comment limited to 30 minutes per meeting
- **Committee of the Whole model:** The entire legislative body acts as one large committee for discussion and debate on county issues. This structure is implemented through two standing committees covering administrative and financial matters.

## Standing committees (COTWs)

Boone County uses the Committee of the Whole structure with two standing committees:

- **COTW – Administrative & Legislative Committee:** Handles administrative and legislative matters. The Chair may recommend revisions to board and committee rules to this committee.
- **COTW – Finance, Taxation & Salaries Committee:** Addresses budget, appropriations, taxation, assessment policy, expenditures, purchases, and financial audits.

Committee meeting schedules and agendas are maintained by the Administration Office. Minutes for County Board meetings are kept by the County Clerk.

## Departments

Boone County operates the following departments and offices:

- **17th Judicial Circuit Court:** Serves both Boone and Winnebago Counties, hearing all case types including criminal, civil, family, probate, and landlord-tenant matters.
- **Administration:** Coordinates with County Board and committees; handles budget, finance, human resources, purchasing, information technology, and inter-department coordination.
- **Animal Services:** Government-owned and operated animal control facility, certified and inspected by the State of Illinois, funded through vaccination registration.
- **Assessment Office:** Oversees fair and equitable assessment of real property, manages assessment exemptions and ownership records.
- **Building & Zoning:** Enforces adopted building and zoning codes; issues building permits, conducts inspections, enforces special use permit conditions, handles zoning complaints.
- **Clerk & Recorder** (Amy E. Ohlsen): Serves as Clerk of the County Board, Keeper of County Records, Chief Election Officer, Keeper of Vital Records (birth, marriage, death records starting in 1877), computes property taxes and handles delinquent tax redemption, records and maintains all land transactions (records start in 1838). Located at 1212 Logan Avenue Suite 103, Belvidere.
- **Clerk of the Circuit Court:** Maintains records of all court cases, collects and disburses court fines and fees, summons jurors for petit and grand juries.
- **Coroner:** Law enforcement agency performing thorough death investigations pursuant to Illinois State Statute.
- **Corrections & County Jail:** Under Sheriff's authority; provides custody for individuals awaiting trial, serving sentences up to one year, or sentenced to periodic imprisonment.
- **Emergency Management (EMA):** Protects lives and property before disasters through planning, training, and mitigation; coordinates local response after disasters.
- **Geographic Information Systems & Maps:** Maintains maps and geographic databases for the entire county; provides public access to property data via GIS website.
- **Health Department:** Provides public health services, clinics, and environmental health services.
- **Highway Department:** Inspects and maintains highways and bridges; issues access, overweight/oversized, and utility permits; develops budgets, designs road projects, reviews subdivision plans and zoning issues, assists townships.
- **Planning Department:** Serves county zoning needs; provides information on Comprehensive Plan, Zoning Code, and Subdivision Code; processes Special Uses, Rezonings, Variances, Text Amendments, and Subdivisions. Provides staff support to multiple commissions and boards.
- **Probation:** Operates under supervision of Chief Judge of the Seventeenth Judicial Circuit; administers services to offenders sentenced to community supervision.
- **Public Defender:** Represents individuals accused of crimes carrying potential jail or prison time who cannot afford private counsel; handles traffic to murder cases, juvenile cases, and abuse/neglect cases.
- **Sheriff's Office:** Protects lives and property of citizens; enforces federal, state, and local laws in unincorporated areas of the county.
- **State's Attorney:** Represents the People of the State of Illinois; prosecutes criminal statute violations occurring in Boone County (except traffic offenses in the City of Belvidere).
- **Storm Water and Pollution Control:** Addresses storm water pollution controls for the Belvidere-Boone County area.
- **Treasurer:** Receives and deposits all county revenues and public monies; mails real estate tax bills, collects taxes, distributes funds to taxing districts; collects 1% food and beverage tax supporting the Community Building Complex.
