## Overview

Boone County is located in northern Illinois, with Belvidere as its county seat. The county spans approximately 282 square miles (281 square miles of land, 1 square mile of water) and had a population of 53,448 as of the 2020 census. It is part of the Rockford metropolitan area.

Boone County operates under a county board form of government, utilizing the Committee of the Whole (COTW) structure for legislative deliberation. The county provides a full range of services through elected constitutional officers and appointed departments, and partners with independent conservation districts that operate within its boundaries. The county government is statutorily governed by 55 ILCS 5 (Illinois Counties Code).

## The County Board

- **Composition:** 12 elected members, four from each of three districts
- **Terms:** Board members serve either two- or four-year terms
- **Leadership:** The Chair and Vice Chair are elected by the members of the board; the Chair has the authority to fill committee vacancies and committee chairmanships
- **Meeting location:** 1212 Logan Avenue – County Board Room
- **Meeting time:** 6:30 PM (regular meetings)
- **Committee of the Whole model:** Boone County utilizes a COTW structure in which the entire board functions as one large committee for the purpose of discussion and debate on various issues before formal action. All board members serve on the two standing committees.

## Departments

The county operates the following departments and offices:

- **17th Judicial Circuit Court** – Serves both Boone and Winnebago Counties
- **Administration** – Business matters including budget, finance, human resources, purchasing, IT; keeps standing committee minutes and records
- **Animal Services** – County-operated animal control, state-certified
- **Assessment Office** – Oversees property assessment and ownership records
- **Building & Zoning** – Enforces building and zoning codes, issues permits, conducts inspections
- **Clerk & Recorder** – Processes legal documents, maintains vital records (birth, death, marriage starting 1877; land records starting 1838), conducts elections; County Clerk serves as Clerk of the County Board
- **Clerk of the Circuit Court** – Maintains court records, collects fines and fees, summons jurors
- **Coroner** – Investigates deaths as required by statute; operates as a law enforcement agency
- **Corrections & County Jail** – Under Sheriff's supervision; custody for individuals awaiting trial or serving sentences up to one year
- **Emergency Management (EMA)** – Pre-disaster planning and post-disaster response coordination
- **Geographic Information Systems & Maps** – Maintains maps and geographic databases for the county
- **Health Department** – Public health services, clinics, environmental concerns
- **Highway Department** – Inspects and maintains highways and bridges, issues permits
- **Planning Department** – Serves zoning needs, administers Comprehensive Plan, Zoning Code, Subdivision Code; provides staff support to planning-related commissions
- **Probation** – Operates under supervision of Chief Judge of the 17th Judicial Circuit
- **Public Defender** – Represents indigent defendants in criminal and certain family matters
- **Sheriff's Office** – Law enforcement in unincorporated areas; constitutional and statutory enforcement responsibilities
- **State's Attorney** – Prosecutes criminal violations occurring in Boone County
- **Storm Water and Pollution Control** – Addresses storm water pollution for Belvidere-Boone County area
- **Treasurer** – Receives and deposits county revenues; mails tax bills, collects taxes, distributes to taxing districts; collects 1% food and beverage tax for Community Building Complex
