{%- comment -%} data-pagefind-body scopes the search index to the summary. The date is carried as metadata because every meeting of a body shares its h1 — without it, twenty meetings of the same board are twenty identical search results. The date goes into the result *title* rather than being rendered by a custom template, because replacing that template would also drop the per-heading sub-results the default one gives ("The lede", "Decisions & votes"), which are worth keeping. The standalone `date` field stays indexed for anyone who does write a template later. One filter per element: inside a data-pagefind-filter an inline `key:value` consumes the rest of the attribute, so two of them on one element silently produce one filter with a garbled value. The body filter uses the parent body's title rather than this page's, which is the meeting's own name and varies ("... , 4:30 PM", "Special Meeting: Board Vacancy Interviews"). Filtering on those would offer 35 choices for 22 bodies, several of which are not bodies at all. {%- endcomment -%}
