## Agenda preview

## The lede

- The Committee of the Whole will receive updates from the Police and Fire departments and the Finance Department
- The committee will consider renewing the city's property, casualty and workers' compensation insurance with Illinois Municipal League Risk Management Association for $805,170, down $81,325 from the only competing proposal
- The Ida Public Library is requesting a tax levy of $895,340 for next year, an increase of $42,635 or 5% over the prior year
- The committee will discuss "Project Hawkeye" and may enter executive session to discuss collective bargaining matters

## Decisions & votes

_No content._

## Discussion items

**Public Safety Updates:** The Police Department and Fire Department are scheduled to provide updates.

**Finance Department Update:** The Finance Department will provide an update.

**Property, Casualty & Workers' Compensation Coverage:** Budget & Finance Officer Sarah Turnipseed is recommending renewal with the Illinois Municipal League Risk Management Association (IML RMA) for 2027. The city has been an IML RMA member since 1981. Following a council request for competitive quotes, a 22-page request for proposals was sent to three firms, but only Robertson Ryan Insurance submitted a proposal. IML RMA's premium is $811,283 (or $803,170 with a 1% early-payment discount by November 13), compared to Robertson Ryan's $884,495 with no early-payment discount available. IML RMA is a self-insurance pool owned by Illinois municipalities with no broker commission. The 2027 premium represents a 1% increase from 2026.

**Ida Public Library Levy Request:** The library is requesting a levy of $895,340, which represents a $42,635 or 5% increase. Under state law, the library board of trustees determines the amount required, and the city council must levy the taxes in that amount. The council must approve a tax levy by the last Tuesday in December.

**Project Hawkeye:** The item will be discussed in city council discussion only. No additional details were provided.

**Executive Session:** The committee may enter executive session to discuss collective bargaining matters under Section 2(c)(2) of the Open Meetings Act.

## Financial / operational notes

The property insurance proposal covers more than 80 city locations, 124 vehicles, equipment inventory, payroll by job class, and eight years of claims history. The IML RMA coverage includes workers' compensation ($451,069), auto liability and comprehensive general liability ($222,563), property ($101,197), auto physical damage ($28,901), and portable equipment ($7,553), plus $2,000 in IML membership dues.
