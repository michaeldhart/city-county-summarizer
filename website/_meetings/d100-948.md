## The lede

- Committee reviewed Strategic Plan Goal 5 (Operational Resources) progress and district scorecard updates
- Discussed purchase of 1,250 Chromebooks for annual device refresh to support 1:1 instructional program
- Reviewed E-rate contracts providing 85-90% discounts on technology hardware and internet services
- Addressed multiple operational items including FY27 budget preparation, treasurer's bond renewal, federal audit reports, and facility use requests

## Discussion items (no vote)

**Strategic Plan Update - Goal 5**: Administration provided monthly review of district progress on Goal 5: Operational Resources using scorecard metrics.

**2026 Chromebook Purchase**: Recommendation to purchase 1,250 Chromebooks for scheduled annual device refresh to maintain current, reliable student hardware supporting 1:1 instructional goals.

**E-rate Items**: Discussion of contracts leveraging E-rate program's 85% hardware discount and 90% internet services discount, with balance funded by Technology Budget.

**Maintenance Grant RFP Recommendation**: Review of maintenance grant request for proposals and recommendations.

**FY27 Tentative Budget Preparation**: Designation of individual responsible for preparing the FY27 Tentative Budget.

**Treasurer's Bond Renewal**: Review of treasurer's bond renewal for 2026-2027 period.

**Federal Programs Single Audit Report for FY25**: Presentation of federal single audit report, management letter, and corrective action plan.

**Regulatory Basis Engagement Audit FY26**: Discussion of audit engagement letter for FY26.

**Approved Depositories**: Review of approved financial depositories list.

**IHSA Membership Renewal**: Discussion of 2026-2027 Illinois High School Association membership renewals for both high schools.

**Sunday Facility Use - BNHS Relay For Life**: Review of facility use request for May 3, 2026 Relay For Life event.

**Excess Property Disposal**: Two items covering disposal of CMS band instruments and BHS athletics equipment.

**Upcoming Secure Entry RFP Results**: Discussion of upcoming secure entry request for proposals results.
