## The lede

- The Park District board approved a tentative budget for fiscal year 2027 and set a public hearing for November 10, 2026, at 4:45 p.m.
- Commissioners approved a tentative tax levy of $4,943,251, a 3.83% increase over the prior year that will not require a Truth in Taxation hearing.
- The board received an update on the planned General Obligation Limited Tax Park Bonds, Series 2026, including a projected issuance schedule.

## Decisions & votes

**FY 27 Budget and Ordinance No. 26-12-08A** — The board approved the fiscal year 2027 budget and appropriation ordinance in tentative form for public viewing. A public hearing is scheduled for November 10, 2026, at 4:45 p.m. Motion by Nord, seconded by Marquardt. All voted yes on roll call.

**Ordinance No. 26-12-08B (Tax Levy)** — The board adopted the tentative 2026 tax levy ordinance establishing the proposed levy at $4,943,251, a 3.83% increase over the prior year. The increase does not trigger the need for a Truth in Taxation hearing. Motion by Nord, seconded by Marquardt. All voted yes on roll call.

**Meeting agenda approval** — The board approved the October 27, 2026, general meeting agenda. Motion by Nord, seconded by Thacker. All voted yes by voice vote.

**Adjournment** — The board adjourned the meeting at 5:25 p.m. Motion by Nord, seconded by Marquardt. All voted yes by voice vote.

## Discussion items

**Bond issuance update** — Executive Director Jen Jacky provided an update on the General Obligation Limited Tax Park Bonds, Series 2026, including a projected schedule for issuance.

**ADA Transition Plan** — Jacky, Graham, and Williams gave an update on progress with the ADA Transition Plan.

**TIF District vs. Enterprise Zone** — Jacky explained the difference between a TIF District and an Enterprise Zone.

**Synergy meeting** — Jacky provided an update on a Synergy meeting.

**Pet scanner article** — The board discussed a pet scanner article as part of board correspondence and noted positive feedback and exposure.

## Financial / operational notes

The proposed FY 2027 budget and appropriation ordinance includes a review of income and expenses with a budget implementation schedule.

The tax levy of $4,943,251 represents a 3.83% increase over the prior year levy.
