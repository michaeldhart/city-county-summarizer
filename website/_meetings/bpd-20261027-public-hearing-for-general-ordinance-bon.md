## The lede

- The Belvidere Township Park District held a public hearing on the board's plan to sell up to $1,375,000 in general obligation bonds to pay for park land purchases, improvements to existing facilities, and outstanding obligations.
- No members of the public attended or commented during the hearing, which lasted five minutes.
- All five commissioners were present for the hearing at the Baltic Mill.

## Decisions & votes

- The board approved the meeting agenda for the public hearing. (Voice vote, all yes; motion by Nord, second by Thacker)
- The board adjourned the meeting at 4:53 p.m. (Voice vote, all yes; motion by Nord, second by Thacker)

## Discussion items

_No content._

## Financial / operational notes

The proposed bond sale of up to $1,375,000 would fund payment for land condemned or purchased for parks, building, maintaining, improving and protecting parks and existing facilities, payment of outstanding district obligations, and related expenses.
