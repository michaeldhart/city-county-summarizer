## The lede
- The City Council approved nearly $2.65 million in expenditures, including $1.99 million for the General and Special Funds and $659,805 for the Water & Sewer Fund.
- The Council unanimously approved three contracts totaling approximately $388,286 for the Main Street Bridge expansion joint replacement and Allen Street Pond outlet project, both aimed at infrastructure repair and flood mitigation.
- During public comment, seven residents spoke in opposition to a proposed data center project (referred to as "Project Hawkeye"), continuing concerns raised at the October 5 meeting.
- The Council accepted a $750 donation from Townhomes of Cedar Village for K9 Unit supplies.

## Decisions & votes

**Resolution #2026-23** – A resolution for improvement under the Illinois Highway Code for the Main Street Bridge expansion joint replacement ($244,500 from MFT funds).  
Vote: 9-0 (Albertini, Brereton, Fleury, Frank, Hoiness, McGee, Montalbano, Peterson, Stevens voting yes; Gramkowski absent)

**CES Inc. construction services contract** – Up to $121,226 for construction services for the Allen Street Pond Outlet to River Project, paid from Capital Funds. The project is part of ongoing efforts to reduce flooding on the south side of town.  
Vote: 9-0 in favor

**Path Construction Company contract** – Low bid of $244,500 for Main Street Bridge expansion joint replacement project.  
Vote: 9-0 in favor

**Hampton, Lenzini & Renwick engineering agreement** – Up to $22,560 for construction engineering for the Main Street Bridge expansion joint repair project, paid from MFT funds.  
Vote: 9-0 in favor

**Donation acceptance** – $750 from Townhomes of Cedar Village for K9 Unit supplies/equipment.  
Vote: 9-0 in favor

**General & Special Fund expenditures** – $1,989,555.17  
Vote: 9-0 in favor

**Water & Sewer Fund expenditures** – $659,804.88  
Vote: 9-0 in favor

**Executive session** – The Council entered closed session at 6:58 p.m. to discuss collective bargaining and pending litigation, returning to open session at 7:20 p.m.  
Vote to enter: 9-0 in favor  
Vote to exit: 9-0 in favor

The Council also approved minutes from the October 5, 2026 meeting and the October 12, 2026 Committee of the Whole meeting.

## Discussion items

Assistant Library Director Daisy Mechler provided an update on the Ida Public Library.

The Council discussed the Allen Street Pond Outlet to River Project during the vote on the CES Inc. contract, noting it is part of a continuing plan to reduce flooding on the south side of town.

## Financial / operational notes

Monthly reports were placed on file for September 2026, including Police Department overtime, Fire Department overtime, Community Development/Planning Department activity, Building Department revenues and permits, the Financial Report, General Fund Report, and Water/Sewer Report. No donations were reported for September 2026.

Minutes from the October 13, 2026 Planning and Zoning Commission meeting were placed on file.

Alderperson Gramkowski was absent from the meeting.
