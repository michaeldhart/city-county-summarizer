## Agenda preview

## The lede
- The City Council will consider approving approximately $2.65 million in expenditures across all funds, including $1.99 million for general and special funds and $659,805 for water and sewer operations.
- The council will vote on a $244,500 bridge repair project for Main Street expansion joints, to be funded through Motor Fuel Tax dollars.
- The agenda includes multiple infrastructure projects forwarded from committee, including a $121,226 contract for the Allen Street Pond Outlet project and construction engineering services for bridge repairs.

## Decisions & votes

The council will consider:

- **Resolution #2026-23**: Approving the Main Street Bridge Expansion Joint Replacement under the Illinois Highway Code for $244,500, paid with Motor Fuel Tax funds.

**Motions forwarded from Committee of the Whole (Public Works):**
- Approving a contract with CES, Inc. for up to $121,226 for construction services on the Allen Street Pond Outlet to River Project, paid with capital funds.
- Approving the low bid from Path Construction Company for $244,500 for the Main Street Bridge Expansion Joint Replacement Project.
- Approving a construction engineering agreement with Hampton, Lenzini & Renwick for up to $22,560 for construction engineering on the Main Street Bridge project, paid from Motor Fuel Tax funds.

**Motions forwarded from Committee of the Whole (Public Safety):**
- Accepting a $750 donation from Townhomes of Cedar Village for K9 Unit supplies and equipment.

## Discussion items

- **Public Comment**: Residents may register with the City Clerk to speak.
- **Special Messages**: The Ida Public Library will present.
- **Monthly Reports**: The council will receive September 2026 reports from the Police Department (overtime), Fire Department (overtime), Community Development/Planning, Building Department (revenues, permits, case reports), and financial reports for general fund and water/sewer operations.
- **Executive Session**: Planned to discuss collective bargaining matters and pending, probable, or imminent litigation.

## Financial / operational notes

- **Total expenditures**: $2,649,360.05 across all funds
  - General fund: $1,528,545.71
  - Special funds: $4,450.48 (Farmington Ponds SSA #2); $962.37 (Farmington Ponds SSA #3)
  - Capital: $455,596.61
  - Water/Sewer: $659,804.88
- **No donations reported** for September 2026.
- Minutes from Planning and Zoning Commission (October 13, 2026) and Committee of the Whole—Building, Planning and Zoning and Public Works (October 12, 2026) will be reviewed.
- Meeting to be presided over by Alderperson Rory Peterson.
