## TL;DR

- Commission processed multiple police officer candidates through various hiring stages including background investigations, polygraph tests, and interviews
- Two candidates (Michael DeFrancisco and Esteban Castanon) passed background checks and were advanced to polygraph testing; two others (Jaleel Bobbitt and Carlee Fridley) failed background investigations
- Neil Rossi successfully completed polygraph and interview, received conditional employment offer pending psychological and medical testing
- Patrick Banka removed from eligibility list for failing Power Test; Kelsey McDonough granted request to be passed over but remain on list
- Commission approved payment of three invoices totaling $990.45 for testing and recruitment services

## Decisions & votes

**Minutes Approval (May 27, 2026):** Approved unanimously (3-0)

**Bills Payable - All approved unanimously (3-0):**
- Rock Valley Publishing invoice for Fire/Paramedic Exam: $155.45
- MHK Solutions pre-employment psychology for Seth Norris: $625.00
- Trotsky Investigative Polygraph services: $210.00

**Background Investigations:**
- Approved Michael DeFrancisco and Esteban Castanon background investigations (3-0)
- Declared Jaleel Bobbitt and Carlee Fridley failed background investigations (3-0)

**Eligibility List Actions:**
- Removed Patrick Banka from #2 position on Consolidated Eligibility List for failing Power Test (3-0)
- Approved Kelsey McDonough's request to be passed over but remain at #4 position on list (3-0)

**Polygraph Authorizations & Results:**
- Authorized Michael DeFrancisco and Esteban Castanon to proceed to polygraph testing (3-0)
- Approved Neil Rossi's polygraph test results (3-0)

**Neil Rossi Hiring Actions - All approved unanimously (3-0):**
- Approved successful completion of interview process
- Extended conditional offer of employment as Probational Officer
- Authorized psychological and medical testing

## Discussion items

Police Chief provided update on current officers' status: Officers Amundson and Drop are in Step #1 training, Officer Sanders is in Step #4, and Officer Norris is in Week #6 at Sauk Valley Police Academy.

Attorney Howland clarified that applicants satisfy statutory requirements as long as they are 34 years old at time of application.

## Financial / operational notes

Total expenditures approved: $990.45 for recruitment and testing services across police and fire departments.

The Commission is actively managing a Consolidated Eligibility List for police officer candidates, with ongoing recruitment and multiple candidates at various stages of the hiring process including academy training.
