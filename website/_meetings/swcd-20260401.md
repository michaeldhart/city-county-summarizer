## The lede
- Natural Land Institute and A3 Environmental Consultants presented on agricultural easement opportunities in Boone County, including discussion of NRCS's ACEP-ALE program that covers 50% of development rights costs
- Board approved PFC (cost share) project rankings for grassed waterways and pollinator habitat, with Jo Daviess County transferring $2,430.87 to Boone County for project funding
- District finances total approximately $533,690 across checking, savings, and Illinois Funds accounts
- NRCS reported EQIP ranking deadline April 3rd, CRP deadline closed, and new Pheasants Forever Biologist starting in Rockford office

## Decisions & votes
- **March meeting minutes approved** (Frank/Aves, passed unanimously)
- **Finance report approved** (Aves/Brahmstedt, passed unanimously) - accounts totaling $533,690.54
- **PFC project ranking approved** (Aves/Ratcliffe, passed unanimously) - two grassed waterway projects and one pollinator project using $15,901.21 in available funds; additional $6,123.48 to be requested from IDOA to cover pollinator project if applicant proceeds
- **Timesheets approved** (Ratcliffe)
- **Meeting adjourned** (Aves/Frank, passed)

## Discussion items
- **Agricultural easement program**: Natural Land Institute (NLI) representatives discussed opportunities for agricultural easements in Boone County; NLI currently holds ~4,500 acres in easements, mostly in Northern Illinois; NRCS ACEP-ALE program pays 50% of development rights value; NLI funding helps with administrative costs and additional easement funding beyond NRCS programs
- **FY26 grant applications**: Applications with IDOA now open; district will begin application this week
- **Infrastructure updates**: IDOT to install district road signs in coming weeks
- **Committee reports**: Development team discussed state solar/wind legislation; stormwater committee establishing foundation (2nd meeting); groundwater committee planning Farm Breakfast for June 27th at Fitzgerald farm and discussing repair/replacement of groundwater education models
- **Envirothon event**: Recent event completed; tentative 2027 dates set at Morton Arboretum
- **Cost share programs**: CPRG contracts due May 1st; I-Cover open signup begins April 15th (preapplication opened April 1st)
- **Erosion control inspections**: Regular inspections completed; additional inspection requested by County Highway Department for housing development on Spring Creek Rd
- **NRCS staffing**: Hiring freeze lifted but critical vacancies limited; districts leveraging shared staffing resources

## Financial / operational notes
- **Account balances**: Midland checking $250,888.56; Midland savings $15,062.95; Illinois Funds $267,739.03 (total: $533,690.54)
- **PFC cost share funding**: $15,901.21 available after Jo Daviess County transfer of $2,430.87; additional $6,000-$6,123.48 may be requested from IDOA for pollinator project
- **NRCS administrative assistance**: $6,675 balance remaining (Q1 & Q2 invoices); all FY25 invoices paid
- **Tree and fish sales**: 12 tree orders totaling $1,020; 10 fish orders totaling $1,918.80 (one additional order expected)
- **IDOA spot check**: One Fall Covers for Spring Savings program spot check completed
