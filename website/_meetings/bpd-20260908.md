## The lede

- The Board of Commissioners swore in Carrie Lenzen as a new board member, filling the vacancy left by Amber Rangel.
- The board approved renewing the district's medical insurance with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois with a 15.4% increase to employee contributions, effective November 1, 2026.
- Commissioners approved the 2027 board meeting schedule, then immediately amended it to move the September meeting from the 7th to the 14th.
- Executive Director Jen Jacky reported on an OSLAD grant update, project budgets, a Special Olympics Hamblock fundraiser, and the 2027 budget.

## Decisions & votes

**Medical insurance renewal** — The board voted to renew the district's medical insurance coverage with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois, using CoyleKiley Insurance Agency as broker, effective November 1, 2026. The renewal includes a 15.4% increase to employee medical insurance contributions. Approved unanimously on a roll call vote.

**2027 board meeting schedule** — The board approved the 2027 meeting schedule unanimously on a roll call vote, then immediately amended it to change the meeting date from September 7, 2027 to September 14, 2027. Amendment approved unanimously.

**Routine approvals** — The board approved the meeting agenda, general meeting minutes from August 10, special meeting minutes from August 10 (with Marquardt voting no), and expense report from August 6 to September 3. All passed on roll call votes.

## Discussion items

**Sports market and feasibility study** — The board discussed scheduling interviews for input on the study but took no action.

**Staff reports** — Executive Director Jen Jacky provided updates on OSLAD grant projects, project budgets, the Special Olympics Hamblock fundraiser, and the 2027 budget. Parks & Recreation Director Tony Williams updated the board on the ice rink project. Administrative Service and Superintendent of Recreation reports were reviewed without discussion.

## Financial / operational notes

The board reviewed the August 2026 income statement without discussion. The expense approval report covered the period from August 6, 2026 to September 3, 2026.
