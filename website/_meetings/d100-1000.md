## Agenda preview

- The committee is set to receive an update on the district's strategic plan, focusing on Goal 5: Operational Resources
- Several facility-related items are on the agenda, including a 2027 maintenance grant project, network-leveling controls, and bids for the District Administration Building
- The committee is scheduled to review financial updates on Phase 1 facility improvement projects and consider integrating Phase 2 projects
- Two activity requests are listed: Sunday gym use at Belvidere North High School for Jr. Thunder Youth Basketball and an activity account for the BNHS HOSA (Health Occupations Students of America) chapter

## Items for decision

- 2027 Maintenance Grant Project Approval
- Network-leveling controls installation, including server and level integration proposals
- Bid Group 5 for the District Administration Building
- Sunday facility use request for the Belvidere North High School gym by Jr. Thunder Youth Basketball
- Activity account request for BNHS HOSA (Health Occupations Students of America)

## Other business

- Strategic plan update focusing on Goal 5: Operational Resources
- Phase 1 financial update on facility improvement projects as of September 30, 2026
- Financial update and Phase 2 integration with Phase 1 projects
- Any other matters that come to the committee's attention
- Next meetings scheduled for November 3 at Meehan Elementary School and December 1 at Belvidere Central Middle School
