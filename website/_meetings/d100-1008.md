## Agenda preview

- The committee is set to review the 2026-27 school district scorecard, which tracks progress toward five strategic plan goals using key performance indicators
- The high school course catalog with revisions for the 2027-28 school year is scheduled for review ahead of board approval in November
- An update on state-mandated units of study for 2026-27 is on the agenda, covering new requirements and their impact on curriculum
- The committee is set to review summative designations and the Illinois Report Card

## Items for decision

- **School District Scorecard** — The scorecard identifies growth targets for key performance indicators tied to the district's five strategic plan goals. Board action will be requested at the October school board meeting.
- **High School Course Catalog revisions for 2027-28** — The board will be asked to approve the revised catalog at the November regular meeting.

## Other business

- **Norms for Collaboration** — Review and potential revision of the committee's collaboration norms, first adopted in the 2022-23 school year
- **ISBE IL Mandated Units of Study Update for 2026-27** — Informational update on state educational mandates; no board action required
- **Annual Review of Summative Designations**
- **Illinois Report Card Recap**
- Academic contracts
- Any other matters
- Future meeting scheduled for December 2
