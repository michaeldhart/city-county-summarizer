## Agenda preview

## The lede (3–5 bullets)

- The City Council will consider first readings of six ordinances, including amendments to development fees, authorization to sell city vehicles and equipment, and three tax levy ordinances for the fiscal year beginning May 1, 2026.
- The council will review expenditures totaling approximately $5.1 million, including $3.2 million from general and special funds and $1.9 million from water and sewer funds.
- Items forwarded from committee include approving the 2027 health insurance renewal, retaining legal counsel for environmental matters related to Landfill #1, and consenting to appoint Jeff Peters to fill a vacancy through June 2027.
- The council will consider a special use permit for a daycare center at 390 W. Chrysler Drive and approve connecting an IDOT facility on Illinois Route 76 to the city's sanitary sewer system.

## Financial / operational notes

The agenda includes approval of $3,159,365.48 in general and special fund expenditures and $1,901,091.12 in water and sewer fund expenditures. Major items include an $800,000 transfer to the capital fund, over $151,000 for Metro Paramedic Services, $522,061 for liability insurance through IML RMA, and approximately $987,000 for Well 11 facility construction. The council will also review monthly reports for police and fire overtime, community development activity, building permits, and financial statements for October 2026. Tax levy ordinances for the fiscal year 2026-2027 will receive first readings, including the general corporate levy and levies for Special Service Areas #2 and #3.
