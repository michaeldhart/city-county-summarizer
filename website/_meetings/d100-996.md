**Approval of 2026-2027 School District Scorecards**

**Norms for Collaboration**
- Committee reviewed collaboration norms first established in the 2022-23 school year, including starting and ending on time, reviewing materials in advance, respecting time-keeping, assuming positive intent, and sharing the air

- Administration presented an overview of adjustments to goals and key milestones for accomplishing growth targets throughout the school year
