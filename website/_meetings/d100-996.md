- Committee reviewed the 2026-2027 School District Scorecard with proposed adjustments to ELA and Math targets for approval
- Early Childhood Program update was presented to the committee
- Administration provided information on ISBE's Accountability Redesign and Summative Designations
- Committee reviewed collaboration norms established in the 2022-23 school year

## Decisions & votes

**Approval of 2026-2027 School District Scorecards**
- The agenda included a recommendation to approve the 2026-2027 District 100 scorecards as submitted with proposed adjustments to ELA and Math targets
- The scorecards identify growth targets for Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) aligned to the five goals of the district's strategic plan
- _Vote count and discussion details not available from agenda materials_

## Discussion items (no vote)

**Norms for Collaboration**
- Committee reviewed collaboration norms first established in the 2022-23 school year, including starting and ending on time, reviewing materials in advance, respecting time-keeping, assuming positive intent, and sharing the air

**2024-2025 School District Scorecard**
- Administration presented an overview of adjustments to goals and key milestones for accomplishing growth targets throughout the school year
- School scorecards were scheduled for review at the October Educational Services Committee meeting with board action requested at the October School Board meeting

**Early Childhood Program Update**
- Staff provided an update on the Early Childhood Program

**ISBE's Accountability Redesign & Summative Designations**
