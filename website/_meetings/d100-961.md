## The lede

- Committee reviewed multiple dual credit and articulated credit agreements with Rock Valley College and Highland Community College for the 2026-27 school year to expand academic opportunities
- Discussed truancy support services agreement with Regional Office of Education at reduced cost of $80,000 (down $112,000 from prior year due to new funding model)
- Reviewed Wellness Center RFP rebid recommendations for abatement work and other construction matters
- Considered multiple operational items including interactive panels for secondary schools, workers compensation insurance renewal, and food service contract renewal with Organic Life

## Attendance & housekeeping

**Members of record:** Jorge Herrera, Holly Houk, Brian Hoemke

**Location:** South Middle School - 919 E 6th St., Belvidere, IL

**Next meetings scheduled:** Monthly through December 2026 rotating among district facilities (May 5 at Belvidere North High School, June 2 at Belvidere High School, etc.)

## Discussion items (no vote)

**Strategic Plan Update - Goal 5:** Administration provided monthly review of progress toward Goal 5: Operational Resources of the district's strategic plan

**Northwestern Illinois Association (NIA) Contract Renewal:** Reviewed contract renewal for FY27 services related to Goal 1: Academic Achievement & Growth

**Running Start IGA with Rock Valley College - SY'27:** Discussed intergovernmental agreement for Running Start program

**Articulated & Dual Credit MOU with Rock Valley College - SY'27:** Reviewed memorandum of understanding for dual and articulated credit courses

**Dual Credit MOU with Highland Community College - SY'27:** Discussed annual agreement for dual credit agriculture courses

**ROE Student Truancy Support Services:** Administration recommended maintaining current service level with Regional Office of Education at $80,000 cost (reduced by $112,000 from SY26 due to adjusted funding model from grant changes)

**Interactive Panels Secondary Schools 2nd Wave:** Reviewed second wave of interactive panel installations for secondary schools

**Granite - POTS Line Replacement:** Discussed replacement of plain old telephone service lines

**Clinic Internet Connection:** Reviewed internet connectivity for clinic facilities

**Panorama: Student Success Platform and SEL Surveys:** Discussed student success platform and social-emotional learning survey tools

**Wellness Center RFP Recommendations:** Reviewed rebid recommendations for Wellness Center abatement work

**FY27 Workers Compensation Insurance Renewal:** Discussed workers compensation insurance renewal for fiscal year 2027

**Organic Life Renewal:** Reviewed food service management company contract renewal for SY26-27

**Cooperative Purchasing Overview:** Discussed cooperative purchasing practices used in District 100

**Excess Property Disposal - Multiple Items:** Reviewed disposal of district-wide radios/batteries/chargers, enVision textbooks, and miscellaneous facilities items

**Activity Account Requests:** Discussed new activity accounts for BNHS Strength & Conditioning Camps/Clinics and BHS/BNHS Special Olympics Team BucThunder student fundraisers

**Sunday Facility Use - PAC Rentals:** Reviewed rental requests from Ms. LeMaster's School of Dance (June 12-14, 2026) and Studio 11 Dance Company (May 31, 2026)

**Moody's Annual Report:** Reviewed annual K-12 report from Moody's rating agency

**RFP Update:** General update on requests for proposals

**Washington & BHS Roof Restoration:** Discussed Bid Package 03 for roof restoration projects at Washington Academy and Belvidere High School
