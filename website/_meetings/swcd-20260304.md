## The lede

- District raised fees for Natural Resources Inventory (NRI) reports: full report base fee increased from $400 to $500, additional acre fee from $20 to $30, and established new wind project fees ($500 for first turbine, $300 per additional turbine)
- Approved $250 donation to the northeastern Illinois Envirothon event for operational support
- Approved NRI 1811 for a 5-acre rezone from A-1 to RE on Spring Creek Road in Belvidere for two private residences, with comments regarding floodplain considerations and buffers
- District has approximately 640 new acres enrolled in cost-share programs for strip till practices over the next 3 years

## Decisions & votes

- **January meeting minutes approved** (Motion: Mike Frank; Second: Aaron Brahmstedt)
- **Finance report approved** showing total funds of $532,517.20 across accounts (Motion: Aaron Brahmstedt; Second: Mike Frank; Passed unanimously)
- **$250 donation to NE IL Envirothon event approved** (Motion: Mike Frank; Second: Jack Ratcliffe; Carried)
- **New NRI fee schedule approved**: Base fee increased to $500 (from $400), additional acre fee to $30 (from $20), wind project fees set at $500 for first turbine plus $300 per additional turbine (Motion: Jack Ratcliffe; Second: Aaron Brahmstedt; Passed unanimously)
- **NRI 1811 approved with comments** for 5-acre rezone on PIN 05-08-200-015, Spring Creek Road (Motion: Mike Frank; Second: Aaron Brahmstedt; Carried)
- **Board organization maintained as-is** (Motion: Jack Ratcliffe; Second: Aaron Brahmstedt; Passed unanimously)
- **Meeting adjourned** (Motion: Aaron Brahmstedt; Second: Mike Frank; Carried)

## Discussion items

- **QuickBooks and grant reporting**: Staff reported on annual subscription increases and expected instructions from IDOA on using Amplifund for future grant reporting
- **Committee meetings**: Staff discussed upcoming meetings for development, stormwater, agriculture, KREP, Envirothon, and groundwater
- **1992 agricultural area review**: Board discussed upcoming review for agricultural area in northern Boone County
- **Document storage**: Bill Hall asked district to research fireproof safe or cabinet for important documents like deeds; staff confirmed district has two easement files on hand
- **Local work group meeting**: Staff suggested holding meeting after April's board meeting similar to Winnebago, rather than joint meeting this year
- **NRCS ranking**: EQIP applications ranking due March 20th; open CRP sign-up with FSA; NRCS assisting with CRP review backlogs in Stephenson and Jo Davies counties; one possible CRP application for Boone
- **Erosion control**: Three active sites in Belvidere under inspection, with Belvidere Retail needing fixes
