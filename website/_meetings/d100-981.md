## TL;DR

- Committee will review the 2024-2025 School District Scorecard tracking progress on strategic plan goals, with school-level scorecards to follow in October
- High schools are seeking a five-year PE waiver to excuse students participating in show choir programs, with a public hearing and board vote scheduled for August
- Class of 2027 graduation ceremonies recommended for May 23, 2027 at BMO Center with Belvidere North at 10 AM and Belvidere High School at 2 PM
- Committee received updates on multilingual programming, summer school recap, and social-emotional supports

## Attendance & housekeeping

The meeting was scheduled for August 5, 2026 at 4:00 PM at 1201 5th Avenue, Belvidere, IL. Members of record: Cassandra Schug, David Carson, Megan Johnson, Jeff Diestelmeier, Scott Mueller, and Jo Ann Armstrong. The committee reviewed its collaboration norms (start/end on time, review materials in advance, respect time-keeping, assume positive intent, share the air), which have been in use since the 2022-23 school year.

## Discussion items (no vote)

**2024-2025 School District Scorecard**: Administration presented the annual scorecard identifying growth targets for Key Performance Indicators across five strategic plan goals. The scorecard tracks district progress throughout the year. School-level scorecards will be reviewed at the October committee meeting with board action requested in October.

**Choir Program PE Waiver Request**: The district is seeking a five-year waiver from Illinois School Code's daily physical education requirement to allow high school students in approved show choir programs to be excused from PE. A public hearing and board approval are scheduled for the August school board meeting.

**2027 Graduation Dates**: The Graduation Venue Selection Committee recommended holding Class of 2027 ceremonies at BMO Center on Sunday, May 23, 2027, with Belvidere North High School graduating at 10 AM and Belvidere High School at 2 PM. Rental agreement and estimated invoice were reviewed.

**Multilingual Program Review**: The committee received an update on multilingual programming.

**Summer School Programming Recap**: Administration provided a recap of District 100 summer school programming.

**Social Emotional Support Update**: The committee received an update on social-emotional support services.

**Agape Therapies and Educational Services Contract Renewal**: Contract renewal for academic services was discussed.
