## TL;DR

- **Meeting minutes overhaul approved**: Board endorsed streamlining committee meeting minutes from detailed transcripts to brief summaries (motions and votes only), saving staff ~30-40 hours/month, with YouTube recordings serving as the detailed record
- **Solar farm damage resolution**: County and Luminous Energy reached agreement on rifle range improvements ($66k) and solar panel damage repairs after bullets from county shooting range struck panels; Luminous will pay for berm upgrades, county will pay panel repairs using $25k escrow already held
- **Unified Development Ordinance advancing**: New zoning code and map moving to Finance Committee next week; Farm Bureau raised concerns about livestock facility setbacks and reverse setback protections, staff agreed to align language with state statute
- **Special use permit approved unanimously**: Storage facility expansion at 3100 US Route 20 approved through planned unit development process with neighbor concerns about trees addressed

## Discussion items (no vote)

**Item 9.04 & 9.05 – Unified Development Ordinance (UDO) and Zoning Map**  
- Will move to Finance Committee meeting June 11, 2026 with planning consultant present
- Winnebago-Boone Farm Bureau presented concerns about livestock facility setbacks and reverse setbacks; staff agreed to align language with state Livestock Management Facilities Act (1,320 ft from occupied residences vs. proposed 500 ft from property lines)
- Farm Bureau requested restoration of reverse setback protections preventing homes from being built too close to existing livestock operations
- Well language removed from A-2 lot requirements because Health Department handles well capping/establishment
- Adult use setback reduced from 3,000 ft to 1,000 ft (still requires special use permit)
- Zoning map hash marks indicate former RC (residential) districts that remain planned unit developments with unique criteria

**Item 9.06 – Poplar Grove Road Bike Lanes IGA**  
- Village of Poplar Grove will pay for bike lane portions within village limits, reducing county's previously approved grant expenditure
- Will return to full County Board for approval vote

**Item 9.07 – Meeting Minutes Policy Change**  
- Board endorsed State's Attorney recommendation to eliminate detailed/near-transcript minutes in favor of brief summaries (motions, votes, key decisions)
- Current practice takes staff 30-40 hours/month; Michelle (Admin) and Amy (County Clerk) both affected
- YouTube recordings provide detailed record; staff can timestamp relevant portions in minutes
- Robert's Rules does not require verbatim transcripts
- Board members discussed AI transcription options but consensus favored simple approach using YouTube as primary detailed record

**Item 9.08 – Landfill #2/Belvidere East Solar Farm Shooting Range Issues**  
- Bullets from county rifle range struck solar panels (photos included in packet)
- Agreement reached: Luminous Energy pays for rifle range berm improvements (~$66k), county pays panel damage repairs using $25k escrow from Ameresco already held in landfill fund
- Justin (Highway) developing specs to raise berms without shortening rifle range distances needed for deputy qualifications
- Rifle range closed until repairs completed
- Original berms built ~25-30 years ago from Route 76 expansion dirt; designed for pistol/shotgun range, not modern rifles

## Public comment

**John Mitchell, President, Winnebago-Boone Farm Bureau** (1925 South Meridian Road, Rockford) presented concerns about Unified Development Ordinance:

- **Livestock facilities**: Requested setback language align with state Livestock Management Facilities Act (1,320 ft from occupied residences, not 500 ft from property lines); requested restoration of reverse setbacks protecting existing livestock operations from nearby residential development
- **A-2 lot wells**: Noted well-related language was removed; Farm Bureau suggested reconsidering requirement that A-2 lots cannot have uncapped/old wells
- **Adult use setbacks**: Current code requires 3,000 ft from schools/churches; proposed reduces to 1,000 ft
- **Technical**: Article numbering issue on page 10 (sections 2.03 and 2.04)
- Mitchell indicated many concerns already addressed in May 29 draft; remained for discussion
