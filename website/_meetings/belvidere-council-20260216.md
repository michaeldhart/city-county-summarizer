## The lede
- City Council passed ordinance updating ward descriptions to reflect annexations since 2011
- Council rejected private IT vendor proposals and approved intergovernmental agreement with Boone County for IT and VOIP services at $110,000/year with 3% annual escalator
- Council approved concept of TIF redevelopment agreement with Dairy Farmers of America; final terms to be presented later
- Rick Menge appointed to Fire & Police Commission to complete Tim Rathburn's term through April 2027

## Decisions & votes
- **Ordinance #751H** (ward boundary updates): Passed 10-0 on second reading
- **Rejection of IT vendor proposals** (Petry Design, Entre Solutions, Sundog): Approved 10-0
- **Resolution #2026-3** (IT/VOIP agreement with Boone County): Adopted 10-0
- **Resolution #2026-4** (IDNR license agreement for storm sewer): Adopted 10-0
- **Resolution #2026-5** (accepting public improvements for MH Subdivision/former Dodge Lanes): Adopted 10-0
- **Resolution #2026-6** (police services IGA with Belvidere Township Park District): Adopted 10-0
- **Resolution #2026-7** (Winnebago-Boone County Investigative Cooperative MOU): **Postponed to March 16, 2026** at Police Chief's request due to potential language changes
- **Dairy Farmers of America TIF concept**: Approved 10-0
- **Rick Menge appointment to Fire & Police Commission**: Approved 10-0
- **Mental health consultant services agreement** (Dr. Christina Warden/Glenwood Center): Approved 10-0

## Discussion items
- Mayor thanked Boone County Board and Chairman Tom Wahberg for improved working relationship on IT agreement
- Director Anderson confirmed TIF balance amount for Dairy Farmers agreement may change by final presentation based on receipts
- Police Chief Woody confirmed Park District Board approved police services agreement on prior Tuesday
- Council discussed not including specific TIF dollar amounts in conceptual motion since final numbers may fluctuate

## Financial / operational notes
- **General & Special Fund expenditures**: $4,780,265.46 approved
- **Water & Sewer Fund expenditures**: $605,730.77 approved
- **Boone County IT/VOIP services**: $110,000 annually with annual increase of higher of CPI-U Midwest or 3%; three-year initial term beginning May 2026
- **IDNR license agreement**: $110/year for 10-year term (May 2026-April 2036) for 21" storm sewer operation/maintenance
- Monthly reports for January 2026 (police overtime, fire overtime, community development, building department revenues/permits, financial reports, donations) placed on file

## Public comment
_No content._

## Notable moments
- Mayor read aloud letter of introduction from Rick Menge detailing his 46-year career at Camcar/Fontana/Acument, 10 years managing city recycling center under Director Anderson, and extensive youth sports coaching experience
- Chief Woody requested postponement of investigative cooperative MOU due to member requesting language changes
- Mayor reminded Council and staff of budget retreat scheduled for Saturday, February 21, 2026 at 8:30 a.m.
- Meeting adjourned at 6:24 p.m.
