- Committee reviewed strategic plan progress for Goal 5 (Operational Resources) and other district goals
- Discussed multiple vendor renewals for FY27, including ParentSquare communication platform, insurance with CLIC, and Frontline financial/HR software
- Considered new Agilex partnership for accounts payable spend analysis and rebate opportunities
- Reviewed disposal of excess property including outdated world language curriculum, damaged instruments, and obsolete textbooks from multiple schools
