## The lede
- Board approved updated Rules and Regulations Ordinance 91 after review by Ancel Glink Law Firm and State's Attorney
- Studio GWA selected for architectural services for Nature Center expansion project at $39,230, chosen over competitors for being local with $8,000 cost savings
- Appropriations Ordinance 159-26 amended to transfer $15,000 from restricted use donations to capital expenses to balance budget after architectural contract award
- Staff completed bridge replacement project at Tuttle Clarkson Natural Area using District lumber, saving $4,000 and creating 50-year lifespan structures

## Discussion items

**Phone System Upgrade**: Executive Director reported solicitation calls causing congestion; phone carrier recommended automated call pickup system to prompt callers to select options

**Architectural Services Selection Process**: Three proposals evaluated by committee of two District staff, one Belvidere Park District staff, and one District 100 staff member. Studio GWA selected as local vendor with $8,000 cost difference versus Nurture. Trustee Hall noted close similarities between top two firms but supported Studio GWA selection

**Executive Session** (4:19-4:34 p.m.): Discussed real property purchase/lease and employee matters; no action taken upon return to open session

## Financial / operational notes

**February 2026 Financial Reports**: Placed on file subject to audit

**Tuttle Clarkson Natural Area Bridges**: Replaced using honey locust trees milled from Distillery Conservation Area lumber; three-week project with 50-year expected lifespan; resulted in $4,000 cost savings

**Controlled Burning**: Completed on 170 acres with seeding to follow in selected conservation areas

**Day of Caring Event**: 52 students from Belvidere North High School scheduled to perform restoration work at Roger's Prairie in Spencer Conservation Area

**Budget Adjustment**: Appropriations Ordinance 159-26 required rebalancing after Studio GWA selection ($39,230 exceeded budgeted $25,000 by $14,230)

**Website Update**: Staff bios added to District website

**Board Attendance**: Lisa Turbyville absent
