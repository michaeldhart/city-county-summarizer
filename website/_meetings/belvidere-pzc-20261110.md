## Agenda preview

The Planning and Zoning Commission will take up a daycare proposal that was postponed from the previous month. The applicant is seeking a special use permit to open a daycare center at 390 West Chrysler Drive in a vacant commercial building that is part of a multi-tenant development. The proposed operation would primarily serve as a before-and-after-school program for about 40 school-age children, with staff helping with homework, social activities and recreation. The site would also include an 8,000-square-foot indoor entertainment center, available to both program members and the public for rentals. An outdoor play area would be installed at the rear of the building, enclosed by a 10-foot-high chain-link fence with concrete bollards to separate it from delivery truck traffic in that area.

The commission was scheduled to hear this case at its October 13 meeting but postponed it after the applicant did not appear. Staff has recommended approval with conditions requiring full compliance with submitted site plans, the tall fence or ceiling structure to contain items within the play area, bollards at least four feet high and 12 inches wide around the outdoor area, and compliance with all other applicable codes.
