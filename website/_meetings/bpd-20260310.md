## The lede
- Board approved 2026 Executive Director goals and adopted administrative ordinances and two new policies (Capital Assets and Surplus Property Disposal).
- Community members presented enthusiastic support for a BMX Pump Track project (estimated $275k, targeting 2027), requesting a development plan including partnerships and donations.
- Board discussed General Mills Park development but requested City Council commitment before proceeding further.
- Accessibility improvements for board meeting entrance are underway with materials ordered and installation expected before the April meeting.

## Decisions & votes
- **Meeting agenda, February minutes, and expense report (02/06-03/05/2026)**: Approved unanimously (motions by Nord, seconded by Thacker/Marquardt).
- **Executive Director 2026 Goals**: Approved unanimously (Nord/Thacker).
- **Administrative Ordinances 26-03-10**: Adopted effective immediately, unanimous approval (Nord/Rangel).
- **Capital Assets Policy and Surplus Property Disposal Policy**: Both policies approved unanimously (Nord/Thacker).
- **Meeting adjournment at 6:06 p.m.**: Approved unanimously (Nord/Rangel).

## Discussion items
- **BMX Pump Track**: Community members (B. Nguyen, G. Rupp, Dr. Cassandra Schug, S. Hammer) presented the project as a positive community enhancement to encourage outdoor activities and address youth anxiety/depression. Estimated cost $275k for design and build; asphalt surface requires less maintenance than dirt trails. School board expressed enthusiastic support. Police suggested bike safety courses. Board requested a development plan including partnerships and donations; project targeted for 2027 capital budget using Pump Trax USA.Com for design.
- **General Mills Park**: PRDMA review update discussed; Board requires City Council commitment before proceeding to next stage.
- **Accessibility Request for Board Meeting Entrance**: Materials quoted and ordered; installation estimated before April board meeting. ADA committee review discussed.

## Financial / operational notes
- February 2026 Income Statement presented (no discussion noted).
- Expense Approval Report covering 02/06/2026 to 03/05/2026 approved.
- Executive Director reported on financial assistance scholarship program update, United Way of Boone County rental update, and market study for possible Indoor Turf Field.
- Parks & Recreation updates: Glenn Green Playground and Kiddle Land upgrades in progress.
