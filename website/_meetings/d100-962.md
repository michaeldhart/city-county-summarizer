## TL;DR

- **BESSA Contract Approved**: Board approved a three-year contract (2026-2029) for the Belvidere Education Support Staff following months of negotiations
- **Major Construction Projects**: Approved roof restoration for Washington Academy and Belvidere High School, and wellness center construction bids following rebid process
- **First Readings**: Four policies advanced for May approval including grading/promotion, student behavior, bus conduct, and PE exemptions
- **Special Education Progress**: District reduced therapeutic school outplacements by 60% over two years; all elementary special education teachers being trained in SLANT literacy program
- **Mental Health Screening Mandate**: State requiring districts to implement mental health screening for grades 3-12 starting in two years; district piloting at Everest High School

## Public comment

**Dan Snow**: Expressed disappointment that six board members voted to close Perry Elementary; thanked Mueller for voting against closure

**Caitlin Lease** (teacher): Detailed the complexity of modern elementary teaching role—simultaneously serving as reading specialist, math instructor, behavior specialist, case manager, and communicator. Emphasized teachers handle diverse student needs academically, emotionally, and developmentally while working behind the scenes on data analysis, planning, and standards alignment. Urged board understanding of full scope of teaching to provide adequate time and support.

**Maggie Garcia** (Central Middle School teacher): Explained Tier 2 pension inequity affecting teachers hired after January 1, 2011. Tier 1 teachers can retire around age 57 with 35 years; Tier 2 teachers must work until 67 for full benefits despite same contributions (~9% salary). Cost-of-living increases smaller and may not keep pace with inflation. Argued this makes teaching less attractive as lifelong career, causing newer teachers to view it as short-term employment (5-10 years), impacting district recruitment and retention.

**Fort Zachary** (ESL teacher): Thanked Mueller for voting against Perry closure and Dr. Eddy for allowing him to transfer to Caledonia. Raised concerns about displaced Perry staff not receiving full list of open positions initially; cited example of math interventionist placed in classroom when interventionist position open. Questioned rumor about administrator filling that role. Stated board lost community trust and integrity over Perry closure process, arguing it bypassed spirit of Illinois school closure statute. Warned of difficulties selling vacant 126-year-old building, predicted new EC center will become financial burden, and anticipated higher taxes. Urged community engagement in local politics and school board elections.

**Andrew Kitson**: Stated board promised to "take care of" Perry teachers but promise was broken within four days when teachers didn't receive full list of open positions. Questioned why math interventionist being placed in classroom when interventionist position available. Referenced rumor about administrator taking that role. Acknowledged board's closure decision is done but emphasized trust and integrity were lost. Asked how board will regain community trust.
