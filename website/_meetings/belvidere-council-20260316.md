## TL;DR
- City Council approved general and special fund expenditures of $2.64 million and water/sewer expenditures of $528,000
- Passed ordinance establishing handicap parking spaces on Garden Drive near old Pope Chiropractic Clinic
- Adopted resolution joining Winnebago-Boone County Investigative Cooperative (renamed from Integrity Task Force)
- Approved multiple infrastructure and public works projects including spring branch pickup, street maintenance program, and wastewater treatment plant change order with $82,393 credit
- First reading of FY27 budget ordinance with reserves of approximately one year

## Decisions & votes

**Ordinance #752H** (2nd reading) - Handicap parking on Garden Drive: **PASSED 9-0**
- Establishes two handicap parking stalls on north side of Garden Drive west of State Street
- For facility purchased by District 100

**Resolution #2026-7** - Winnebago-Boone County Investigative Cooperative MOU: **PASSED 9-0**
- Joins multi-jurisdictional law enforcement cooperative for investigating officer-involved incidents
- Name changed from "Integrity Task Force" for legal reasons

**Resolution #2026-8** - Genoa Road jurisdictional transfer IGA with Boone County: **PASSED 9-0**

**2026 Spring Branch Pickup:** **PASSED 9-0**

**2026 MFT Street Maintenance Program:** **PASSED 9-0**

**WWTP Sludge Heater Replacement Project - Change Order #1:** **PASSED 9-0**
- Credit of $82,393.34 (unusual credit change order)
- Project not yet complete, awaiting gas safety items

**Illinois DCEO Regional Site Readiness Program Grant - additional cost:** **PASSED 9-0**
- City portion: $61,540

**Work authorization amendment - CES, Inc. engineering modifications:** **PASSED 9-0**
- $48,900 from Capital Funds for Regional Site Readiness Grant Project

**Re-establish City-County Coordinating Committee:** **PASSED 9-0**
- To meet quarterly
- City expects to field three aldermen as representatives

## Discussion items

**FY27 Budget Ordinance (Ordinance #753H)** - First reading only; changes from committee packet:
- Budget Officer Sarah Turnipseed removed operating transfer dollars from Exhibit A, page 1
- Revenue reduced to $25,830,395
- Ending cash balance now $23,638,514 (approximately one year of reserves)
- Changes made after consultation with auditors

**City-County Coordinating Committee:**
- Mayor noted county has different fee structure (paid per meeting), creating past incentive for unnecessary meetings
- Three aldermen have expressed interest in serving
- Awaiting county action on re-establishment

## Financial / operational notes

**Expenditures approved:**
- General & Special Fund: $2,642,372.56
- Water & Sewer Fund: $527,946.05
- Specific items questioned: $6,830 to Automotive Solutions for equipment in 2026 Dodge Durango; $11,173 to Arthur Gallagher for underground fuel tank liability insurance

**FY27 Budget:**
- Reserves maintained at approximately one year
- Methodology changes per auditor guidance (operating transfers removed from revenue presentation)

**WWTP Sludge Heater project:**
- Rare credit change order of $82,393.34
- Director Anderson noted credits are uncommon
- Final change order may still include small additional expenses

**Grant modifications:**
- Regional Site Readiness Program: additional $61,540 city match
- Engineering amendment: $48,900 for design modifications

## Public comment

Mayor Morris read thank-you letter from Boone County Fair Association for donation of 1967 Joy Air Compressor.

## Notable moments
