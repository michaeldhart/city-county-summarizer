## Agenda preview

## The lede
- The Boone County Conservation District will consider its annual tax abatement and levy ordinances at its November 17, 2026 meeting.
- The board will review a compensation study presented remotely by Dr. Victoria McGrath of McGrath Human Resources Group.
- The board is scheduled to enter executive session to discuss employee matters including appointment, employment, compensation, discipline, performance, or dismissal.

## Decisions & votes

The board is scheduled to vote on:
- Approval of Abatement Ordinance 160-26
- Approval of Levy Ordinance 161-26
- Approval of Truth in Taxation Certificate of Compliance
- Approval of October 20, 2026 board meeting minutes

## Discussion items

The board will discuss:
- Updates to the hunt program fee schedule
- A compensation study to be presented via Zoom by Dr. Victoria McGrath of McGrath Human Resources Group
- The annual tax abatement ordinance and levy ordinance
- The Truth in Taxation Certificate of Compliance

## Financial / operational notes

The board will review:
- Financial reports for October 2026
- Accounts payable

The board will hear reports from the executive director, director of land and facilities, director of natural resources, and director of education.
