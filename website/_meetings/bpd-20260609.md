## TL;DR
- Board approved revised compensation of $27,150.00 for IDOT's de minimis acquisition related to Route 20 expansion affecting Glenn Green park property
- Budget preparation is on track with areas of concern including utility and health insurance cost increases
- Board discussed OSLAD (Open Space Lands Acquisition and Development) grant application for Winterroth Park development
- Public input included appreciation for Park District programs and activities

## Decisions & votes
- **Meeting agenda (06/09/26):** Approved unanimously on voice vote (Nord/Thacker)
- **General Meeting Minutes (05/12/26):** Approved unanimously on voice vote (Nord/Marquardt)
- **Expense Approval Report (05/08/26 to 06/01/26):** Approved unanimously on roll call vote (Nord/Marquardt)
- **IDOT Route 20 Expansion - Glenn Green De Minimis Acquisition:** Approved unanimously on roll call vote (Nord/Thacker) - revised compensation of $27,150.00 with authorization to execute updated IDOT documents upon General Counsel review
- **Adjournment at 5:19 p.m.:** Approved unanimously on roll call vote (Nord/Thacker)

## Discussion items
- **OSLAD Application:** Discussion covered number of applications allowed, grant application guidelines/requirements for the State of Illinois, and Winterroth Park development
- **Budget Timeline:** Budget preparations are on schedule with staff working on budgets; identified areas of concern include utility cost increases and health insurance cost increases; grant funding discussed

## Financial / operational notes
- **May 2026 Income Statement:** Reviewed with no discussion noted
- **Deer Hills/Deer Woods Impact fees:** Discussed in Executive Director report
- **IAPD expenditure analysis results:** Mentioned in Executive Director report
- **Capital Projects:** Glenn Green playground project completed with temporary parking installed, receiving positive public feedback; Ice Rink project underway; ADA accessible ramp project at upper/lower Doty
- **Recreation Programs:** Youth athletic programs showing growth
