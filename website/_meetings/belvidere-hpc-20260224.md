## TL;DR

- The commission is planning its 2026 awards program and scheduling fundraising events including a scavenger hunt (April), stained glass window tour (July), and Victorian picnic (September)
- Approximately $2,500 is available for property maintenance grants; discussion about lead abatement concerns for window wrapping projects
- Planning for "Friends of Preservation" meetings in April with an open Q&A format rather than formal presentations
- Staff provided updates on downtown façade renovations and new businesses in Belvidere

## Decisions & votes

- **Approved January 27, 2026 minutes** (L. Kummerow/Monaghan) — passed 6-0 by voice vote

## Discussion items

- **2026 Awards Program**: Staff encouraged commissioners to consider potential recipients for the annual preservation awards
- **Fundraising event scheduling**: Commission discussed avoiding May/June due to Heritage Days and graduations; staff will email to confirm availability for proposed dates (Scavenger Hunt: April 18 or 25; Stained Glass Window tour: July 18 or 25; Victorian Picnic: September 19 or 26)
- **Event details**: Noted that stained glass windows from old Methodist Church are in storage; live music suggested for picnics; Boone County Conservation District has costumes from Pioneer Days festival
- **Friends of Preservation format**: Agreed attendees should come with questions rather than having set programs; April dates to be determined via email
- **Community partnerships**: Parks and Conservation Foundation project on historic events at Conservation District properties; potential content for History Keeper newsletter; possible future presentation by Carol Rowe
- **Elections**: Upcoming commission elections mentioned

## Financial / operational notes

- **Property Maintenance Grant budget**: Approximately $2,500 available to award
- **Lead abatement concern**: Question raised about window wrapping eligibility due to lead paint regulations; staff to review specific wrapping methods
- **Book royalties and sales**: No updates reported
