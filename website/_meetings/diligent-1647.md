## Agenda preview

- The commission is set to consider rezoning three lots in Mallard Point Subdivision from single-unit dwelling to residential exurban district
- A preliminary plat for State Solar Subdivision is scheduled for review
- The commission is scheduled to approve minutes from its May 19, 2026 meeting
- Public comment is scheduled

## Items for decision

**Rezoning - MA-09-2026**  
The commission is set to take up rezoning lots 19, 20, and 21 of Mallard Point Subdivision from R-1 (Single-Unit Dwelling) to R-E (Residential Exurban District). Supporting materials include a replat, natural resources inventory, letters of support, and a memo regarding locations on Saddle Path.

**Preliminary Plat - PP-11-2026**  
The commission is scheduled to review a preliminary plat for State Solar Subdivision. Materials include the preliminary plat with IDOT comments and revisions, and correspondence regarding highway department and IDOT road access circulation.

**Minutes approval**  
The commission is set to approve minutes from May 19, 2026.

## Other business

- Public comment is scheduled
- Unfinished business
- Discussion
- Communications, including notice that the next meeting is scheduled for October 20, 2026
