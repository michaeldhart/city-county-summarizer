## Agenda preview

**The lede**

- The Belvidere Township Park District scheduled a public hearing for November 10, 2026 at 4:45 p.m. to gather public comments on the proposed fiscal year 2027 budget and appropriation ordinance
- The hearing was set to take place at the Historic Baltic Mill on West Lincoln Avenue
- The board planned to vote on the budget and Ordinance No. 26-12-08A at its December 8, 2026 general meeting
- A closed session was scheduled following the public hearing
