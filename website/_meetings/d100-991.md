## The lede

- Committee reviewed progress on Strategic Plan Goal 5 (Operational Resources) as part of ongoing monthly updates
- Discussed renewal of Google Workspace licenses for the district
- Reviewed HVAC-related items including secure entry change order and selection of computer controls partner
- Considered disposal of excess property including outdated Spanish curriculum materials and a facilities grounds trailer

## Attendance & housekeeping

**Members of record:** Jorge Herrera, Holly Houk, Brian Hoemke

## Discussion items (no vote)

**Strategic Plan Update - Goal 5:** Monthly review of the district's progress toward achieving Goal 5 (Operational Resources) of the strategic plan, presented by Jo Ann Armstrong. The update included the district's scorecard and schools' scorecard showing implementation progress.

**Google Workspace Renewal:** Recommendation to renew the district's Google Workspace licenses.

**Secure Entry HVAC Change Order:** Informational item regarding change order and reallocation of funds related to secure entry HVAC work.

**HVAC Computer Controls Partner:** Discussion of network level building controls integrator selection through RFQ process, including review of submissions.

**Excess Property Disposal - Envision Spanish Curriculum:** Review of out-of-use Envision Spanish curriculum materials proposed for disposal.

**Excess Property Disposal - Facilities Grounds Trailer:** Discussion of disposal of a facilities grounds trailer no longer needed by the district.

**Facility Phase 2 Discussion:** Review of Phase 2 facility matters.
