## The lede

- Committee reviewed District progress on Strategic Plan Goal 3 (Family and Community Partnerships) with focus on community feedback and potential partnership opportunities
- Administration presented on vape detectors installed at Belvidere North High School and updated student cell phone/electronic device policies
- Committee reviewed results of the 2025-26 Possip District Sentiment Family Survey and universal school supply lists for grades K-8 for 2026-27
- District handbook revisions for 2026-27 school year discussed, with final updates pending July legislative session before August Board approval

## Attendance & housekeeping

**Members of record:** Frank Mandera (Director of Quality Assurance)

The meeting was scheduled for Friday, May 8, 2026 at 12:00 PM at 1201 5th Avenue, Belvidere, IL. The agenda included introductions of committee members and review of committee expectations regarding conduct, time management, and participation protocols per Board Policy 8:30.

## Decisions & votes

_No content._

## Discussion items (no vote)

- **Strategic Plan Progress:** Dr. Schug and Jeanine Hadley presented updates on District progress toward Strategic Plan Goal 3 (Family and Community Partnerships). Committee was asked for feedback on the team's work, next steps, and suggestions for potential community partnerships to explore.

- **Vape Detectors at Belvidere North High School:** Administration reviewed implementation of vape detection systems and standard operating procedures for 2025-2026.

- **Student Cell Phone/Electronic Device Policy:** Updated policy on student behavior regarding cell phones and electronic devices was presented.

- **Family Survey Results:** Committee reviewed the 2025-26 Possip District Sentiment Family Survey results.

- **Universal School Supply Lists:** Supply lists for grades K-8 for the 2026-27 school year were reviewed.

- **District Handbook 2026-27:** Administration discussed upcoming revisions to the Parent/Student Handbook. Final revisions will incorporate July 2026 legislative changes and be brought to the Regular Board Meeting in August 2026. The handbook includes updated District policies and connections to PRESS (Policy Reference Education Subscription Service) policies from the Illinois Association of School Boards.

- **Future meeting dates** for the 2026-27 school year were discussed.
