## The lede

- The board approved an updated Letter of Assurance for the Funderburg South Conservation Area wetland mitigation bank project, committing $222,750 in operations or reserves in case of default, though the long-term management fund requirement was reduced from $122,000 to $78,122.
- The board authorized Secretary Ken Hall to sign wealth management documents with Midland States Bank, a routine update required every three years.
- The board reviewed plans for an eagle-viewing pull-off along Woodstock Road and heard that fall tree planting will include seven native species as part of a state grant project.
- The board met in closed session to discuss purchasing or leasing property but took no public action afterward.

## Decisions & votes

**August 2026 meeting minutes** – Approved 3–0 (Turbyville, Sanford, Hall).

**Funderburg South wetland mitigation bank Letter of Assurance** – Approved 3–0 (Sanford, Turbyville, Hall). The updated letter commits $222,750 of district operations or reserves if the district defaults on its commitments; the long-term management account requirement was lowered to $78,122 from the original $122,000, while the initial fund amount of $18,841 remained unchanged.

**Wealth Management Corporate Authorization** – Approved 3–0 (Turbyville, Sanford, Hall), allowing all trustees and the executive director to sign the document.

**Wealth Management Investment Policy Statement** – Approved 3–0 (Turbyville, Sanford, Hall), authorizing Secretary Hall to sign.

**Executive session** – The board voted 3–0 (Sanford, Turbyville, Hall) to enter closed session to discuss purchasing or leasing property.

**Accounts payable through September 15, 2026** – Approved 3–0 (Sanford, Turbyville, Hall).

**Adjournment** – Approved 3–0 (Turbyville, Sanford, Hall) at 4:30 p.m.

## Discussion items

**Woodstock Road bridge and eagle-viewing pull-off** – Executive Director Josh Sage showed the board a map from County Engineer Justin Krohn of the proposed eagle-viewing pull-off along Woodstock Road, created at the board's earlier request.

**Executive session** – The board discussed purchasing or leasing property in closed session but took no public action.

## Financial / operational notes

**August 2026 financial report** – Placed on file subject to audit.

**Fall tree planting** – Director of Natural Resources Aarron Minson reported the fall planting for the LIB CRTI Grant Project will include Burr Oak, Hill's Oak, Speckled Alder, Hazelnut, Wild Plum, Witch Hazel, and Iowa Crab, with dormant-season planting followed by winter snow insulation and spring rains to help establish the trees.

**Volunteer hours** – Director of Land & Facilities Mark Freedlund reported August 2026 volunteer hours were updated. He also promoted the 2026 Fall Celebration event.

**Board attendance** – Treasurer Bob Phelps and Board President Meredith Williams were absent; Secretary Ken Hall chaired the meeting.
