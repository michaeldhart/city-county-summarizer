## Agenda preview

## The lede
- The Boone County Conservation District is scheduled to meet September 15, 2026, with a lengthy agenda that includes a potential land purchase.
- The board will review an updated Letter of Assurance for a wetland mitigation bank project at Funderburg South Conservation Area involving the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers.
- The board is set to discuss and potentially approve investment policy changes, including authorizing Secretary Ken Hall to sign an Investment Policy Statement.

## Decisions & votes

The board agenda includes potential votes on:

- Approving an updated Letter of Assurance for the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers wetland mitigation bank project at Funderburg South Conservation Area
- Approving Secretary Ken Hall's signature on an Investment Policy Statement related to wealth management investment objectives
- Approving the August 18, 2026 meeting minutes
- Potential action on a land purchase following a closed executive session

## Discussion items

- **Funderburg South Conservation Area wetland mitigation bank**: The board will discuss an update to the Letter of Assurance for a project with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers
- **Woodstock Road Bridge**: An update is scheduled for discussion
- **Wealth management investment objectives**: The board will discuss investment policy matters
- **Executive session**: The board plans to meet in closed session to discuss buying or leasing real property for public use, with potential action to follow

The board will also hear reports from the Executive Director, Director of Land & Facilities, Director of Natural Resources, and Director of Education.

## Financial / operational notes

The board will review financial reports for August 2026 and approve accounts payable.
