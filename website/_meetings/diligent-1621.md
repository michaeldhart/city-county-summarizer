## The lede
- Board conducted election of Chair and Vice Chair positions as unfinished business
- Voted on SUP-04-2026 for Huber Road Assembly, a special use permit application with both support and opposition submitted
- Approved variation Z-08-2026 for 11132 Woodstock Road property
- Received update on Unified Development Ordinance and Zoning Map progress

## Attendance & housekeeping
Members of record: Carl Rudy, Cory Javurek, Michelle Marella, Nate Sroka, Rick Schultz, Erin Marshall

The board approved the agenda and minutes from the May 26, 2026 meeting. Next meeting scheduled for July 28, 2026.

## Decisions & votes
**Election of Chair and Vice Chair** – Carried over from unfinished business; no vote details available from agenda.

**SUP-04-2026, Huber Road Assembly (8410 Huber Road)** – Special use permit application for an assembly use. Extensive documentation submitted including site plans, health department materials, food handler certificates, environmental impact letter, and both supporting and opposing comments. Application includes conservation and building elements with full site plan review.

**Z-08-2026, 11132 Woodstock Road** – Motion to approve variation for property at 11132 Woodstock Road. Application included site documentation and environmental review.

_Vote counts and specific discussion details not available from agenda materials._

## Discussion items (no vote)
The board received an update on the Unified Development Ordinance and Zoning Map as part of planning reports.

## Public comment
Public comment period was included on the agenda. Both support and opposition materials were submitted for the Huber Road Assembly special use permit.

_No content._
