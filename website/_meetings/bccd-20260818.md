## The lede

- The board reorganized its leadership structure, naming Meredith Williams as president, Ken Hall as secretary, and Bob Phelps as treasurer, with Lisa Turbyville and Rachel Sanford serving as trustees.
- The board approved a scaled-down concept design for a nature center expansion project, reducing the size from 4,500 to 3,500 square feet to stay within a $3 million budget, and authorized applying for an OSLAD grant to fund it.
- Following an executive session, the board voted to retain Attorney Wendi Wade as legal counsel through January 2027 to complete Attorney Deb Loos's contract.
- The district received a clean audit opinion for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, which the board approved.

## Decisions & votes

**Amended meeting agenda** to add language about discussing personnel matters in executive session. Passed 5-0.

**Approved July 21, 2026 meeting minutes** as presented. Passed 5-0.

**Restructured the board** with Meredith Williams as president, Ken Hall as secretary, Bob Phelps as treasurer, and Lisa Turbyville and Rachel Sanford as trustees. Passed 5-0.

**Approved the annual financial report** for the year ending March 31, 2026. Passed 5-0.

**Approved committee assignments**, including Rachel Sanford to Parks & Conservation Foundation, Bob Phelps and Ken Hall to License Review Committee, Lisa Turbyville to Risk Management Committee, Ken Hall to Finance Review Committee and Board of Trustees Email, Ken Hall and Bob Phelps to Finance Review Committee, and other assignments. Passed 5-0.

**Approved the nature center expansion project concept design** created by Studio GWA for the fiscal year 2027 OSLAD grant submission. Passed 5-0.

**Approved Resolution 08-2026A** authorizing submission of an OSLAD grant application for the nature center expansion project and authorizing the board president and secretary to sign the resolution. Passed 5-0.

**Approved updating bank signature cards** for all district accounts. Passed 5-0.

**Voted to enter executive session** at 4:46 p.m. Passed 5-0.

**Retained Attorney Wendi Wade** as district legal counsel to complete Attorney Deb Loos's contract through January 2027. Passed 5-0.

**Approved accounts payable** through August 18, 2026. Passed 5-0.

**Adjourned the meeting** at 5:15 p.m. Passed 5-0.

## Discussion items

Executive Director Josh Sage updated the board on public survey responses collected during an open house and at the Boone County Fair about the nature center expansion project. He said all public feedback had been positive.

Attorney Deb Loos introduced Attorney Wendi Wade to the board and district staff.

Hunter Cejka reported that the automotive lift at the maintenance facility was inspected on August 13, 2026, and that bridge spans were picked up on August 18, 2026.

Mark Freedlund thanked everyone who helped staff the Boone County Fair booth.

Rachel Sanford introduced herself as a lifelong Boone County resident who has been volunteering at the district since 2021 with her husband.

Jeremy Homola addressed the board during public comment to say he missed the payment deadline for the 2026-2027 archery program and wanted to pay and participate. He said he has participated in the district's program for 12 years and in McHenry County's hunt program for 15 years, and offered suggestions to improve the district's hunt program.

The board discussed plans for Attorney Deb Loos's going away party after the September meeting.

## Financial / operational notes

The July 2026 financial report was placed on file subject to audit.

Lindsey Fish from Sikich CPA LLC presented the annual audit for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026. Fish said the district received a clean audit opinion. Lisa Turbyville thanked Executive Director Sage for his help with the audit process.

The nature center expansion project was reduced from 4,500 square feet to 3,500 square feet to stay within a $3 million budget.
