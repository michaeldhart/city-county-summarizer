## The lede
- Board approved staff participation on regional stormwater technical committee and chairman participation on policy committee, with first meeting expected late February
- Total district funds across all accounts: $523,370.94 (checking, savings, and Illinois Funds)
- NRCS application deadline for CSP and EQIP programs is January 15th
- District reviewing NRI policy and fees; updates expected at next meeting

## Decisions & votes
- **December meeting minutes approved** (moved by Mike Frank, seconded by Todd Aves)
- **Finance report approved unanimously** (moved by Jack Ratcliffe, seconded by Aaron Brahmstedt) - Total funds: $243,065.72 in Midland checking, $15,060.35 in Midland savings, $265,244.87 in Illinois Funds
- **Staff participation on regional stormwater technical committee and Chairman Bill Hall participation on Stormwater Committee (policy) approved unanimously** (moved by Jack Ratcliffe, seconded by Todd Aves)
- **Meeting adjourned** (moved by Mike Frank, seconded by Todd Aves)

## Discussion items
- **Regional stormwater committee**: First technical committee meeting scheduled for late February
- **Wind energy projects**: Directors discussed wind project solicitations occurring in southern and northern Boone County
- **Tree planter**: District's co-owned tree planter currently stored at county highway yard
- **PFC cost share programs**: Updates provided on CPRG and NRCS administrative agreement work for USDA programs
- **NRCS programs**: Staff working on CSP payments and CRP site visits for renewals in Boone and Stephenson counties
- **New Regenerative Ag program**: USDA program to be carried out via EQIP and CSP, requires extended soil testing and minimum 5-year contract
- **IDOA Conservation Planner vacancy**: No updates on filling the position; NRCS will inquire

## Financial / operational notes
- Jack Ratcliffe approved timesheets
- NRI (Natural Resources Inventory) policy and fees under review; fee updates to be presented at next board meeting
- Application deadline for CSP and EQIP: January 15th
