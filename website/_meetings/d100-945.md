## The lede

- Board to vote on district vision statement: "Community rooted. Every child included. Learning, innovating, and leading — thriving today and prepared for tomorrow."
- Budget deficit update presented; district addressing anticipated shortfall for coming school years
- Facilities Master Plan advanced with funding discussion, polling questions for community input, and next steps presentation
- Matt Fry appointed as Principal at Everest High School for 2026-2027 school year
- Mathematics and World Language curriculum resource adoptions on consent agenda

## Attendance & housekeeping

Members of record: Barbi Yunk, Jermaine Pigee, Scott Mueller, Holly Houk, Jorge Herrera, Jeff Diestelmeier, Brian Hoemke

Meeting called to order at 6:00 PM at 1201 5th Avenue, Belvidere, IL. Board entered closed executive session to discuss security procedures, personnel matters, collective bargaining, real property purchase/lease, and student discipline before reconvening in open session.
