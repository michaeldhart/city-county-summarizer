## The lede

- Board scheduled to discuss and provide feedback on a proposed new district vision statement: "Community rooted. Every child included. Learning, innovating, and leading — thriving today and prepared for tomorrow."
- Nexus Solutions to present Phase 1 update on the Facility Master Plan, including priority projects, D100 Wellness Center relocation for Everest High School expansion, and Perry Elementary upgrades
- Board expected to approve Audio Enhancement RFP for $614,179.44 for audio/intercom safety improvements
- District celebrated Belvidere North High School's AP Honor Roll Bronze Recognition and a Golden Apple finalist

Vote expected on banking services request for proposals.

**Audio Enhancement RFP**
Vote expected to approve Audio Enhancement RFP response for $614,179.44 for audio/intercom safety improvements.

## Discussion items (no vote)

**District Vision Statement**
The proposed vision statement — "Community rooted. Every child included. Learning, innovating, and leading — thriving today and prepared for tomorrow" — was presented for Board review and feedback. The vision was developed over the past year with input from staff, families, students, and community partners. Following Board input, the statement may be refined and brought back for formal approval in February.

**Facility Master Plan Phase 1 Update**
Nexus Solutions scheduled to review Phase 1 progress, including first-round priority needs projects from the Facility Needs Assessment, relocation of the D100 Wellness Center to enable Everest High School expansion, and upgrades to bring Perry Elementary School to Tier 2. The presentation included project breakdowns by site and timelines. The district is preparing for Phase 2, which may include an Early Childhood Center and additional priority projects.

**Upcoming Contract Renewals & Reports**
Administration presented a list of annual reports and contracts, with plans to add contracts exceeding $25,000 for regular Board review. Future contracts under consideration include capital expenditures, curriculum renewals, content filtration, Microsoft renewal, ClassLink integration, iStation, Elevation, and Learning A-Z.
