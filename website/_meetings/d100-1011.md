## Agenda preview

- The board is scheduled to hold a public hearing on raising the driver's education fee from $250 to $400
- The district's FY 2026 financial audit is set to be presented to the board
- First readings of 28 district policies are on the agenda, covering topics from employee ethics to student services
- The board is scheduled to consider a tax levy for 2026 and receive a sales tax update
- Recognition items include celebrating Board Member Appreciation Day, student musicians selected for a regional festival, and both cross country teams qualifying for state championships

## Items for decision

- Approve a resolution requesting state code modifications to allow increasing the driver's education fee from $250 to $400
- Adopt revisions to Policy 7:30 on student transfers
- Approve the high school course catalog with revisions for the 2027-28 school year
- Approve elementary interventionist/ESL workload plan and special education case management/workload plan
- Consider a memorandum of agreement with BESSA regarding vacation payout
- Approve revisions to the administrative handbook
- Review consent items including minutes, personnel report, financial reports, bills, an updated memorandum of understanding with Boone County Health Department, excess property disposal, Sunday facility use requests, overnight field trip requests to California and Rosemont, fundraiser requests, and donations

## Other business

- Committee reports from Business Services (Nov 4), Educational Services (Nov 4), and Policy & Personnel (Nov 5)
- Student council reports from Belvidere High School and Belvidere North High School
- Discussion of IASB Delegate Assembly resolutions
- Review of upcoming contract renewals
- Administrative reports including strategic plan updates, state-mandated units of study for 2026-27, Illinois Report Card recap, summative designations review, concussion protocol, district organization chart, and school activity reports
