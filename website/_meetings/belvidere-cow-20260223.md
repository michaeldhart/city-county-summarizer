## The lede

- Police Department progressing well with new officer training; two officers completed FTO program and are now providing shift coverage
- Fire Department apparatus bay floor to be refinished using higher bidder ($34,390) due to significantly shorter completion time (2 days vs. 10 days)
- Approved multiple street closures for Chamber of Commerce events: Buchanan Street Markets (3 dates), Downtown Trick-or-Treat (Oct 28), and Hometown Christmas (Dec 4)
- Two handicap parking spaces approved for 1414 North State Street (former chiropractic clinic, now District 100 wellness facility)

## Decisions & votes

**Approved:** License agreement between City and Franklin Display Group, Inc. for SWAT training at 910 E. Lincoln Ave. in March 2026  
_Vote: Unanimous approval_

**Approved:** Station 2 apparatus bay floor refinishing contract to TSR Concrete Coatings for $34,390 (capital fund), rejecting lower bid of $29,969 from AA Concrete Works due to completion time (2 days vs. 10 days)  
_Vote: Unanimous approval_

**Approved:** Buchanan Street Markets street closures for May 29, June 19, and July 31, 5:00-9:00 PM; closures from 4:00-9:30 PM for Buchanan Street (State to Main) and Whitney Boulevard; trash receptacles at designated locations  
_Vote: Unanimous approval (voted separately)_

**Approved:** Downtown Business Trick-or-Treat event October 28, 5:00-7:00 PM; street closures 3:00-7:30 PM (State Street from Lincoln to 1st, Logan Avenue between State and Main); trash receptacles at six locations  
_Vote: Unanimous approval (voted separately)_

**Approved:** Hometown Christmas event December 4, 4:00-9:00 PM; street closures 3:00-9:30 PM (State Street from Lincoln to 1st, Logan Avenue from State to Main, 1st Street from State to Garfield); trash receptacles at 10 locations  
_Vote: Unanimous approval (voted separately)_

**Approved:** Ordinance establishing two handicap parking spaces on north side of Garden Drive at 1414 North State Street  
_Vote: Unanimous approval_

## Discussion items

**Police staffing progress:** Officers Austin Smaha and Ethan Shook completed step 5 of Field Training Officer program and now provide independent shift coverage. Three other officers remain in various FTO phases; three more attending basic academy at Rock Valley/Dixon. Entry-level eligibility list now has 8 candidates with one opening to fill. Two spots confirmed at Police Training Institute with deadline approaching.

**Fire apparatus bay floor project:** Chief Schadle emphasized operational concerns with extended project timeline—10-day closure would significantly increase disruption and apparatus exposure to weather. Aldermen agreed that finding a reliable 2-day weather window is more feasible than 10 days, especially in March with potential snow/inclement weather.

**Chamber events:** Downtown Trick-or-Treat attracted nearly 7,000 visitors last year; Hometown Christmas draws approximately 12,000 visitors annually. Events designed to increase foot traffic to local businesses.

**Handicap parking:** City Attorney Drella noted the improvements actually make parking better than it has been for decades when the property was Pope Chiropractic clinic.

## Financial / operational notes

**Fire Department call volume (past 30 days):**
- 282 total calls (9.4/day); 72.3% EMS responses
- 179 ambulance transports: 38% to local hospital, 36% to St. Anthony's, 13% to Mercy, 12% to UW Rockford
- Major incidents: 3 fires, 3 gas leaks, 2 CO releases, 2 power lines down, 1 elevator rescue, 1 vehicle-into-house requiring shoring, 9 vehicle collisions with injuries
- Serious medical: 1 cardiac arrest, 3 strokes, 3 overdoses, 4 seizures, 17 breathing problems, 10 chest pain, 5 unconscious persons, 4 hemorrhage/lacerations

**Fire Department admin assistant resigned unexpectedly; good response with résumés received**

**Fiscal Year 2026 Third Quarter Financial Snapshot:**
- Tax revenue remaining strong and steady compared to FY24 and FY25
- Ambulance services operating deficit: $460,000 (lower than budgeted)
- General fund expenses: $19,639,928 (78% of budget)
- General fund revenues: $24,326,239 (96% of budget)
- General fund tracking exactly as planned

**Station 2 apparatus bay floor project:** Cure time after 2-day installation is 48 hours. Project must be completed before fiscal year end. Station will be out of service with apparatus staged outside during work.
