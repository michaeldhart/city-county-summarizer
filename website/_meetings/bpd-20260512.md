## The lede
- **Commissioner resignation accepted**: Board accepted Commissioner Rangel's resignation and established a process to fill the vacancy by appointment until the next election.
- **Glenn Green Playground officially named**: The playground will be named the "Jean Twyning Nature Playground" with a ribbon-cutting ceremony scheduled for May 28, 2026.
- **New software and legal services approved**: Board approved purchase of RecTrac App Software ($8,600+) and engaged Attorney Hursh for ordinance violation representation.
- **Santa House acquisition**: Approved intergovernmental agreement with City of Belvidere to acquire and transfer the Santa House for up to $750 in transportation costs.
- **Officers re-elected**: All officers retained their positions for 2026-2027.

## Decisions & votes

**All motions passed unanimously with roll call or voice votes:**

- Approved meeting agenda, April 14 meeting minutes, and expense report (April 3 - May 7, 2026)
- Named Glenn Green Playground the "Jean Twyning Nature Playground" and authorized permanent signage
- Accepted Commissioner Rangel's resignation and adopted Resolution No. 05-12-26 establishing board appointment process to fill vacancy
- Approved engagement agreement with Attorney Hursh of Guyer & Enichen for legal representation on ordinance violations
- Approved purchase of RecTrac App Software from Vermont Systems for $8,600 plus annual Apple Developer License fee
- Approved disposal of Round One Surplus items for 2026
- Approved de minimis land acquisition for IDOT Route 20 expansion with authorization to request 10% compensation increase (from $26,300 to $28,930); item returns to Board in June if IDOT denies increase
- Approved intergovernmental agreement with City of Belvidere for Santa House acquisition with District costs not to exceed $750 for transportation
- Re-elected slate of officers: President D. Noble, Vice President A. Nord, Secretary M. Marquardt, Treasurer G. Thacker
- Approved committee assignments: G. Thacker for Boone County Arts Council; D. Noble for Parks & Conservation

## Discussion items

- **Glenn Green Playground**: Thacker spoke with Jean Twyning regarding the naming; ribbon-cutting ceremony scheduled for May 28, 2026
- **Board vacancy process**: Board will review applicants and appoint someone to fill Commissioner Rangel's position until the next election
- **RecTrac App Software**: Cost review conducted before approval
- **IDOT land acquisition**: Legal counsel provided update on the de minimis acquisition
