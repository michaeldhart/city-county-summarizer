## Agenda preview

- The committee is set to review a comprehensive update to district policies from the Policy Reference Education Subscription Service (PRESS), including 27 policy revisions responding to recent state legislation
- A new policy on wireless communication devices is scheduled for consideration, which would prohibit student use of phones and similar devices during school time with certain exceptions
- The committee is set to review workload plans for elementary interventionists, ESL staff, and special education case managers
- Updates to the strategic plan's Goal 4 (Premier Workforce) are scheduled for presentation
- The committee is set to review an updated organizational chart and revisions to the administrative handbook

## Items for decision

No formal votes are listed on this agenda; the committee is set to review materials that may be forwarded to the full board for action.

## Other business

**PRESS policy updates (27 policies)**: The committee is scheduled to review policy changes responding to multiple 2026 state laws, including:
- Policies addressing new requirements for cardiac response drills, jury duty compensation, and glucagon administration
- Updates changing "foreign language" to "world language" throughout curriculum policies
- Revisions adding menopause-related conditions to pregnancy protections in employment and student policies
- Changes to policies on student discipline, including prohibitions on expelling K-2 students except for specific weapons offenses
- A new policy (7:195) on wireless communication devices that would ban student use during school time with exceptions for health, safety, individualized education programs, and classroom instruction

**Policy updates from legal reference review (4 policies)**: Updates addressing artificial intelligence use in teacher evaluations, transportation of homeless students, and teacher salary advancement for continuing education

**No-change policy review**: 22 policies reviewed with no recommended changes

**Section 4 annual review**: Annual review of fiscal and business management policies

**Personnel items**: Review of district organizational chart; workload plans for interventionists, ESL teachers, and special education staff; BESSA memorandum of agreement on vacation payout; administrative handbook revisions

**Next meeting**: December 3, 2026
