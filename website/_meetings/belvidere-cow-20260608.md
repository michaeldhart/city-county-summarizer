## TL;DR

- **Public Recognition:** Fire Chief Shawn Schadle honored for 20 years of service; Ed Buell recognized for his 98th birthday
- **Equipment Purchases:** Council approved purchase of pavement saw ($21,132) and Bobcat utility vehicle ($80,195) for city departments
- **Fire Department Grant:** Approved execution of $20,180 state fire equipment grant with additional $2,965 from capital funds for SCBA fill station
- **Zoning Compliance:** Annual zoning map publication approved after extensive database cleanup revealed discrepancies dating back decades

## Decisions & votes

**All votes were unanimous unless otherwise noted:**

- **Resolution directing publication of official zoning map** – Approved
- **Purchase of Husqvarna FS 3500G pavement saw from Esch Construction Supply** – Approved at $21,132.12, funded from water depreciation account (61-1780)
- **Purchase of 2026 Bobcat UW56 utility vehicle from Bobcat of Rockford** – Approved at $80,194.84, funded from depreciation line item 61-1790 ($79,355.04) and sewer expense line item 61-5-820-6030 ($839.80)
- **Execution of FY26 Small Equipment Grant Agreement** – Approved for $20,180 state grant
- **Purchase of SCBA fill station and cascade storage system from Air One Equipment** – Approved at $23,145 total ($20,180 from grant, $2,965 from capital funds)
- **Block party request for Riverview Lane (July 25, 2026, 3-6 p.m.)** – Approved

## Discussion items

- **Building Department Updates:** 146 permits issued (15 commercial, 131 residential), including Dutch Bros, school wellness facility, Walmart cold storage, and potential car wash. Property maintenance has 25 cases in progress.
- **Planning Department Updates:** Two variance applications for Central Gardens; four special use applications pending (oversized garage, impound yard, video gaming, and a mural celebrating the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence). Six to seven additional cases expected for July agenda.
- **Zoning Map Corrections:** Extensive database cleanup revealed discrepancies between municipal boundaries, zoning layers, and taxing databases. Saint James Cemetery discovered to have been within city limits since 1910 but recorded as county property. Approximately a dozen properties corrected across all three database layers.
- **Water Department:** Well number seven passed testing and scheduled to go online June 9, 2026. Current pavement saw cuts unevenly; old saw will be auctioned after council approval.
- **Public Works Transition:** Director Brent's last day; new assistant director Nicole started previous week.
- **Executive Session:** Scheduled discussion on collective bargaining matters postponed for future meeting.

## Financial / operational notes

- **FY27 Water Department Budget:** Included $50,000 allocation for pavement saw; actual purchase came in at $21,132.12
- **FY27 WWTP Budget:** Included $80,000 for utility vehicle with attachments; final cost $80,194.84 (split between depreciation and expense line items)
- **Fire Department Grant:** Originally believed grant application was unsuccessful; later received approval notification. Grant covers $20,180 of $23,145 total equipment cost. Original May 4th council approval did not include the four 6,000 PSI UN/ISO cascade storage cylinders ($6,600), which are essential system components.
- **Sourcewell Cooperative Contract:** Bobcat purchase made through cooperative contract 020223-CEC
- **Building Permits:** Commercial permits totaled approximately $20,000; residential permits approximately $40,000

_No content._

## Notable moments

- **20-Year Service Recognition:** Mayor presented Fire Chief Shawn Schadle with formal recognition for 20 years of service (anniversary June 5, 2026). Chief expressed gratitude for community support and noted it has been "a great honor and blessing" to serve Belvidere.
- **Birthday Recognition:** Mayor publicly wished Ed Buell a happy 98th birthday (June 9).
- **Public Works Leadership Transition:** Director Keck acknowledged outgoing director Brent's 42 years of service and described him as "a great mentor" over the past three years.
- **Grant Application Confusion:** Chief Schadle explained the unusual sequence where council approved the fill station purchase in May believing the grant was denied, only to receive grant approval notification one week later.
- **Emergency Communications Concern:** Mayor noted discussions with Chrysler regarding the importance of emergency response systems that allow fire department communication between interior and exterior command during incidents.
