## Agenda preview

## The lede

- The Board of Commissioners will consider issuing up to $1,375,000 in bonds for park purposes and to pay off certain obligations.
- Riley Magnuson will be introduced as the new Special Recreation Supervisor.
- The board will review updates on the FY 27 budget and appropriation ordinance.

## Decisions & votes

The board is scheduled to vote on:

- The meeting agenda for November 10, 2026
- General meeting minutes from October 27, 2026
- Public hearing meeting minutes from October 27, 2026
- Expense approval report covering October 9 through November 5, 2026
- Ordinance No. 26-11-10 authorizing the sale of up to $1,375,000 in General Obligation Limited Tax Park Bonds, Series 2026, for park purposes and paying certain outstanding district obligations, with a direct annual tax levy to pay principal and interest

## Discussion items

The board will receive:

- An income statement from October 2026
- Staff reports
- An update on the FY 27 budget and Ordinance No 26-12-08A (Annual Budget & Appropriation Ordinance)
- Board correspondence
- Public input

The agenda notes no update will be provided on Ordinance No 26-12-08 B (Annual Tax Levy Ordinance).

## Financial / operational notes

The bond ordinance would authorize borrowing up to $1,375,000 for park purposes and to pay off outstanding obligations, with repayment funded through a direct annual tax levy.
