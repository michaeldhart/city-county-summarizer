## The lede
- The Board of Fire & Police Commissioners planned to review entry-level police officer applications, including polygraph results, interviews, and conditional job offers for multiple candidates.
- The board scheduled consideration of appointing a lateral (experienced) police officer applicant to a probationary position after reviewing their polygraph, medical exam, and peer review.
- One entry-level applicant requested to be bypassed in the hiring process but remain on the eligible list.
