## Agenda preview

## The lede
- The Committee of the Whole will consider draft tax levy ordinances for the fiscal year beginning May 1, 2026, with the corporate levy unchanged from the prior year and not including the library's requested 5% increase.
- The committee will review a school district request for new stop signs on East 6th Street at Perrsons Parkway near Meehan Elementary School, despite a Public Works traffic study finding no need for additional traffic controls based on current data.
- The committee will discuss the 2027 health insurance renewal and a request from the Illinois Department of Transportation to connect its Belvidere facility at 9797 Illinois Route 76 to the city's sanitary sewer system.

## Decisions & votes

_No content._

## Discussion items

**Badge presentation**: The agenda includes a presentation of a Belvidere Police Department badge during the public forum.

**Building and planning updates**: Both the Building Department and Planning Department are scheduled to provide updates. The committee will consider an ordinance amending development application fees for zoning and subdivision matters, creating a new simplified process for consolidating small parcels.

**Intersection safety review**: The Public Works Department determined that the East 6th Street and Perrsons Parkway intersection does not warrant additional traffic controls based on traffic volume (2,200 average daily trips on 6th Street versus 200 or less on Perrsons), only one accident in five years, and no sight distance problems. However, Belvidere School District 100 has formally requested installation of stop signs on East 6th Street to create a three-way stop, citing numerous near-miss incidents with pedestrians, a student struck by a vehicle approximately four years ago, traffic congestion during school hours, and motorists frequently ignoring crossing guards.

**Surplus equipment sale**: The Public Works Department will seek authorization to sell seven surplus vehicles and equipment items, including tractors, trucks, saws, an excavator and a wheel loader.

**IDOT sewer connection**: The Illinois Department of Transportation is requesting approval to connect its Belvidere facility outside city limits to the city sanitary sewer system, replacing an existing septic system with gravity piping, a grinder lift station and force main.

**Tax levy discussion**: The committee will review three tax levy ordinances for fiscal year 2027: the main corporate levy, and levies for Special Service Area #2 and Special Service Area #3. The corporate levy remains at the same level as the prior year and does not incorporate the Ida Public Library's requested amount of $895,340, which would be a 5% increase.

**Health insurance renewal**: The 2027 health insurance renewal is listed for discussion.

**Library board appointment**: Mayor Clinton Morris is appointing Jeff Peters to fill an unexpired position on the Ida Public Library Board through June 30, 2027, replacing Maegan English who resigned.

**Generator donation**: The Fire Department applied for and is seeking approval to accept a donated Generac iQ5200 dual fuel portable inverter generator from Generac Gives at no cost to the city, to be used for portable lighting, battery charging and small electrical equipment at emergency scenes.

**Executive session**: The committee plans to meet in closed session to discuss collective bargaining matters and employee matters.

**Legal representation**: The committee will consider retaining Martin & Bell LLP to represent the city on environmental matters related to the landfill.

## Financial / operational notes

The draft tax levy for fiscal year 2027 shows total expenditures of $66,852,374 across all funds, with $6,582,392 to be raised by property tax levy. Major components include the General Fund ($36,163,222), Water/Sewer Fund ($15,102,450), and capital projects ($6,484,440).

Development application fees under the proposed ordinance would range from $600 to $700 base fees depending on zoning district and use type, plus $75 per acre. Subdivision preliminary and final plat fees would be $500 plus $75 per lot for residential and $600 plus $75 per lot for commercial properties. A new simplified parcel consolidation fee is set at $200 for residential and $300 for office/commercial/industrial properties.

Special Service Area #2 and Special Service Area #3 would each levy a tax not exceeding 0.12% of full fair cash value of taxable property within their respective areas.
