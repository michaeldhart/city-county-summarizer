## Agenda preview

## The lede
- The Boone County Conservation District will consider approving three sets of meeting minutes, including two from executive sessions dating back to September 2025.
- The board will hear a presentation on the Long Prairie Trail Historic Agricultural Crossing from Renee Keeney.
- Board members will review preliminary levy estimates and discuss a ComEd soil boring geotechnical project.
- The board plans to enter executive session to discuss pending litigation and employee matters, with potential action to follow on litigation issues.

## Decisions & votes
The board is scheduled to consider:
- Approving minutes from executive sessions held March 18, 2026 and September 16, 2025, plus the September 15, 2026 board meeting
- Approving Resolution No. 10-2026A (no details provided in agenda)
- Taking potential action following executive session regarding litigation

## Discussion items
- Long Prairie Trail Historic Agricultural Crossing presentation by Renee Keeney
- ComEd soil boring geotechnical project
- Preliminary levy estimates presentation

## Financial / operational notes
- The board will review the September 2026 financial report
- Board members will be presented with preliminary levy estimates
- Staff reports are scheduled from the Executive Director, Director of Land & Facilities, Director of Natural Resources, and Director of Education
- The board will review accounts payable
