## The lede

- Committee reviewed multiple facility improvement projects including BNHS woodshop remodel, Meehan playground upgrades, and secure entrance projects (scopes 3 & 4 rebid plus abatement work)
- Discussed expansion of therapy dog programs from BNHS to include a proposal for SMS (Seth Whitman Middle School)
- Considered IT infrastructure upgrade (Axis server replacement) and disposal of excess property from multiple schools
- Reviewed new activity account requests for SMS Tennis, BNHS Mental Health Club, and BNHS Girls' Golf Student Fundraiser

## Attendance & housekeeping

Members of record: Jorge Herrera, Holly Houk, Brian Hoemke

Meeting location: 9393 Beloit Rd, Belvidere, IL 61008 - Forum Room

Next meeting scheduled for June 2, 2026 at Belvidere High School

## Discussion items (no vote)

- **Strategic Plan Update - Goal 5:** Administration provided monthly review of progress on Goal 5: Operational Resources, including district and school scorecards
- **BNHS Facility/Therapy Dog Update:** Update on existing therapy dog program at Belvidere North High School and review of proposal to expand therapy dog program to SMS
- **Axis Server Replacement:** Discussion of server replacement proposal for technology infrastructure
- **Meehan Playground:** Review of playground improvement proposals from multiple vendors
- **BNHS Woodshop Remodel:** Discussion of bid tabulations for woodshop renovation project
- **Secure Entrances Scopes 3 & 4 Rebid:** Review of rebid results for secure entrance projects at multiple schools
- **Secure Entrances Abatement:** Discussion of abatement work bid results related to secure entrance projects
- **RFP Update:** General update on status of various requests for proposals
- **Activity Account Requests:** Review of new activity account requests for SMS Tennis, BNHS Mental Health Club, and BNHS Girls' Golf Student Fundraiser
- **Excess Property Disposal:** Discussion of disposal requests for SMS balance beams, BNHS fitness equipment, BNHS folding machine, Facilities kitchen mixer and air compressors, and Technology Department equipment
