## Agenda preview

- The board is set to consider approval of minutes from five meetings in August, including two special meetings and one administrative hearing
- The board is scheduled to review financial reports and approve claims paid during the previous month
- The board has items on the agenda regarding a building update, Board Docs/Diligent software, and draft goals for the Public Health Administrator for fiscal year 2027
- Reports are scheduled on flu shot programs, an upcoming free flu clinic, and the health department's 60th anniversary open house
- The board may enter executive session
