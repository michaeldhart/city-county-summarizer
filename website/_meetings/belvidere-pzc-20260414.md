## TL;DR

- Approved text amendments cleaning up zoning ordinance definitions, numbering, and regulations for dwelling types, fencing, parking, and subdivision improvements
- Approved special use for gaming machines and bar inside Citgo gas station at 717 N. State Street with 10 conditions including ADA compliance and health department approval
- Approved rezoning of 13-acre Rush Power Systems site from Rural Holding to Planned Industrial District
- Approved special use planned development for Rush Power Systems with 10 deviations including outdoor storage, wider driveways, crushed asphalt surfacing, and sidewalk waiver

## Decisions & votes

**Case 2026-01 (Text Amendment)**: Approved 5-0 to recommend approval of zoning and subdivision ordinance text amendments addressing dwelling type definitions, cultivation parking exemptions, parking stall dimensions, fencing on through lots, public hearing procedures, subdivision variances, building setback lines, and subdivision improvement timeframes.

**Case 2026-02 (Kullar/Citgo Special Use)**: Approved 5-0 to recommend approval with 10 conditions for indoor commercial entertainment (bar and video gaming) at 717 N. State Street. Conditions include limiting gaming area to 120 square feet (17'x7'), maximum 5 gaming machines, solid barrier with designated entrance, ADA compliance, 6 striped parking stalls, health department compliance, and architect-stamped drawings required before permits.

**Case 2026-04 (Rush Power Systems Rezoning)**: Approved 5-0 to recommend rezoning approximately 13 acres at 1014 Irene Road from Rural Holding to Planned Industrial District.

**Case 2026-05 (Rush Power Systems Special Use)**: Approved 5-0 findings of fact and approved 5-0 to recommend approval with 4 conditions for planned development with deviations including outdoor storage, cultivation over 20% of lot, flexible landscaping placement, delayed street frontage landscaping, 40-foot driveway width, 110-foot flare width, crushed asphalt for outdoor storage, sidewalk waiver, and native plantings over 8 inches.

**Minutes**: Approved December 9, 2025 minutes 6-0.

## Discussion items

Commission discussed clarification on fencing amendments regarding arterial streets and through lots for Case 2026-01.

Commissioner Gnewuch questioned whether green markings on site plan represented parking spaces for Case 2026-02. Staff confirmed handicap space would be added on north side where none currently exist, and obstructions shown would be removed. Setbacks confirmed already in place.

Commission asked about gaming license status; staff reported 36 of 50 available licenses currently in use with some expiring soon.

Applicant Kullar expressed concern about cost of architect-stamped plans required as condition. City Attorney clarified engineer-stamped plans required for building permits. Clarified food service not required for gaming, but health department approval needed for on-site alcohol consumption.

For Rush Power Systems cases, applicant explained company is generator service provider for hospitals and government agencies, currently employing 16 people with plans to hire additional technicians.

Staff reported several potential projects anticipated and 2 cases scheduled for May 12, 2026 meeting.
