## Agenda preview

- The board is scheduled to meet in closed session to discuss the possible purchase or lease of real property
- A resolution to accept a written offer to purchase school real estate is on the agenda for possible action
- Public comment is scheduled with a three-minute speaking limit per person

## Items for decision

- Approval of the agenda
- Possible action on a resolution to accept a written offer to purchase school real estate

## Other business

The board is scheduled to begin in closed executive session under the Open Meetings Act to discuss whether a particular parcel of real property should be acquired for district use. After reconvening in open session, the board is set to review meeting norms and board protocols and hear from visitors during public comment.
