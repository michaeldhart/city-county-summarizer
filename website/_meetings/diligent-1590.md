## TL;DR
- County Board approved three change orders totaling $56,723 for courthouse/health department projects, including winter construction measures to accelerate completion by two months to August 2026
- Board approved new three-year IT services agreement with City of Belvidere for $110,000 annually with annual increases of 3% or CPI (whichever is greater)
- Residents urged immediate action on dangerous Orth and Calonia Roads intersection, requesting four-way stop after recent serious accident; matter referred to February 5 administration committee
- Circuit Clerk collective bargaining agreement approved (December 2025–November 2028); negotiations concluded quickly with wages as primary issue

## Attendance & housekeeping
Members present: Karl Johnson (Chair), Josh Shoemaker, Brian Schneider, Ryan Curry, Alisa Patterson, Rodney Riley (Vice-Chair), Matt Ragano, Marion Thornberry. Members participating remotely (approved by board vote): Tom Walberg, Dave Wiltse (experienced technical difficulties, joined late). Members absent: Dave Cordray, Dana Logsdon.

Meeting called to order at 6:32 p.m. Agenda amended to remove item 11.2 (courthouse change order) and add 9.2 (project update). Minutes from October 16 and November 20, 2025 approved.

## Decisions & votes (each with vote count and notable discussion or dissent)

**Appointments** (all approved unanimously on consent)
- Norman Stimes appointed to Capron Rescue Squad

**Collective Bargaining Agreement - Circuit Clerk's Office** (Dec. 1, 2025–Nov. 30, 2028)
- Approved unanimously (10-0 on roll call)
- Circuit Clerk noted negotiations concluded quickly in December; wages were primary negotiation topic

**IT Agreement with City of Belvidere**
- Approved unanimously (10-0)
- $110,000 annually; 3-year term; annual increases of 3% or CPI (whichever greater)
- Board members expressed support for cooperation with city; agreement goes to Belvidere City Council on Monday

**Ordinance 26-03 - Property Tax Abatement of Alternate Bonds Series 2015A**
- Approved unanimously (10-0 on roll call)

**Claims Approval - $1,990,192.57**
- Approved unanimously (10-0 on roll call)
- Chairman Johnson abstained from claims by William Charles, Northern Illinois Service Co., Core & Main, Highway Department, and county departments regarding sewage/storm water/construction/excavation
- Finance noted $1,162,000 of total was annual Surma liability/workers comp insurance

**Change Order 12 - Scandroli Construction ($10,246.92)**
- Winter conditions for Health Department south addition
- Approved unanimously (11-0 on roll call)
- Discussion: Would reduce construction timeline by approximately 67 days (2 months) if approved with Change Order 13; allows August completion vs. October/November
- Cost offset by approximately $5,000/month savings on Midland rental space

**Change Order 13 - Scandroli Construction ($14,678.84)**
- Winter conditions for Administration Building east addition
- Approved unanimously (11-0 on roll call)
- Works in conjunction with Change Order 12; contractor advised both needed to achieve full 2-month timeline reduction

**Change Order 14 - Scandroli Construction ($31,797.88)**
- Health Department roof work, storm water drainage, exterior wall repairs
- Approved unanimously (11-0 on roll call)
- Addresses water infiltration causing masonry deterioration on north wall
- Solution: Remove two interior roof drains, repitch 16 feet of roof to drain to existing gutter
- Replace deteriorated concrete masonry units and damaged window
- Total project cost $43,797.88 less $12,000 masonry restoration allowance already in contract = $31,797.88

**CCS 2026 Owner's Rep Services Proposal**
- Approved unanimously (11-0 on roll call)

**Ordinance 26-05 - Animal Services Fee Schedule Update**
- Approved unanimously (11-0 on roll call)

**Motion to Enter Executive Session (litigation)**
- Approved unanimously (11-0 on roll call)

## Discussion items (no vote)

- Dan Seedra and Randy Darnell (CCS) provided progress report
- Courthouse: Front entrance arches now visible; interior departments (probation, CASA) moved into new spaces; EOC nearing completion; Courtroom #1 ready for walkthrough Monday
- Transit building: Construction documents submitted to IDOT; awaiting approval to release for bids
- McKinley Avenue project: Final piece completes end of January when coroner moves in
- Public Safety Building basement remodel: Nearing completion, on track for next month
- All five projects on schedule, within budget, quality exceeds industry standards

**Community Agency Reports**

- **Growth Dimensions**: 2025 had 1,200 business engagements, 150 active projects, 2.5M+ sq ft development, 3,600+ new jobs, $4M+ investments; 76 projects for 2026; enterprise zone had 13 new projects ($2.3M investment); website engagement up 60%

## Public comment

**Intersection Safety Concerns - Orth and Calonia Roads**
- **Juliet Pinkston** (12805 Prairie Meadow Way): Requested four-way stop at Orth/Calonia intersection; travels it 4+ times daily for school runs; nicknamed "Dead Man's Crossing" as teenager; cited recent serious accident Friday evening with jaws of life extraction, helicopter transport, driver who caused accident suffered severe brain injury; noted population growth (ISC tournaments, new subdivisions: Prairie Meadow, Newberry Place, Prairie Lane, Cross Creek Estates)

- **Kelly Clark** (12784 Vestus Place): Echoed safety concerns; personally slows when approaching from north/south despite no stop sign due to safety fears; intersection angle creates blind spots making gap judgment difficult; argues four-way stop wouldn't meaningfully burden traffic; noted speed feedback signs recently installed don't address stop sign compliance; emphasized duty of care, requested action before next fatality

- **Board discussion**: Member Shoemaker noted intersection has steep incline on one side creating traction issues in wet/icy conditions; drivers commit to crossing while still gaining traction; compared to Worth Road which has multiple stops along North Road; suggested greater need than Dawson Lake/76 intersection which received stop sign; noted Van Lars grocery store development will increase traffic; urged action without lengthy study process; Chairman referred matter to February 5 administration committee meeting
