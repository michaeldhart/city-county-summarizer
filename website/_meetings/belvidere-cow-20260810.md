## TL;DR
- City approved purchases of two 1-ton dump trucks totaling ~$210K for water and street departments, plus equipment repairs and attachments using remaining capital budget funds
- Union Pacific Railroad pipeline crossing agreement approved for $20K to install storm water pipes under tracks as part of $5.9M Fifth Avenue flood control project
- City attorney explained township dissolution is not legally feasible without county action; discussion of vehicle parking enforcement challenges
- Liquor code amendments approved to allow cash bars at catered events and clarify outdoor beer garden permits
- Buchanan Street Market rescheduled to September 5, 2026 due to weather

## Decisions & votes

**Approved unanimously (all motions passed by voice vote):**

## Discussion items

**Building & Planning Updates:**
- 48 code enforcement cases in progress; 3 ticketed
- $127,392 in permits issued (10 commercial, 140 residential)
- Multiple vacant parcel zoning verifications suggest potential housing development uptick
- FEMA redrawing flood plain maps with significant impact on developable land

**FEMA Flood Plain Map Crisis:**
- New maps designate 100-year flood plain on property west of Stellantis (previously targeted for supplier park)
- City and Region One have invested years and grants preparing infrastructure (water, sewer extensions)
- Land bank purchased property specifically for industrial development
- Director submitted formal comments questioning FEMA's data and methodology
- Multiple other properties affected including General Mills, DFA, and Columbia area parcels
- Maps impact both north and south of Chrysler facility, potentially undermining economic development plans

**Township Government Discussion (Alderman Stevens):**
- Belvidere residents pay ~$1.4M annually to township, receive ~$300K return for roads
- Township reportedly has $8M in bank with minimal services provided
- City Attorney explained dissolution legally impossible without county board action creating co-terminous boundaries
- Would require state legislative change or countywide referendum eliminating all townships
- Strongest option: work with legislators to introduce enabling legislation

**Vehicle Parking Enforcement:**
- Complaints about vehicles not moving, leaking fluids on streets
- Current code: vehicles must move every 24 hours
- City Attorney advised against distance requirements; enforcement challenges include tire-marking constitutionality questions
- Leaking fluids already illegal and enforceable
- Council discussed balancing enforcement costs against actual public harm

## Financial / operational notes

**Capital Budget Management:**
- Street Department had $750K for four rolling stock pieces; purchased for $708,534.34
- Remaining $41,465.66 allocated to tractor repair and loader attachments
- Original $150K 10-yard dump truck budget: purchased used 10-yard and 5-yard trucks for $100K total, redirecting $50K savings to offset street department 1-ton truck cost overrun

**Fifth Avenue Storm Sewer Project:**
- FY2027 budget: $5.9M total project
- Two 36" ductile iron storm water pipes in 48" steel casings
- Outlet from detention pond to Kishwaukee River
- Project includes completed low-flow channel and detention pond already reducing flooding

**Fee Structure:**
- Caterer retailer license: $200 annual endorsement (requires base Class M license at $1,750 renewal, $20K initial)
- Special event licenses: limited to 3 per year per location
