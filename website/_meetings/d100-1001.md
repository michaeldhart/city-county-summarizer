## Agenda preview

- The committee is set to review the 2026-2027 district scorecard, which tracks progress on strategic plan goals, along with individual school scorecards aligned to district priorities
- A waiver request is on the agenda that would allow the district to hold three full days of school improvement planning instead of six half days
- The committee is scheduled to receive data on early college credit programs, including dual credit courses, Advanced Placement courses, and the Running Start program
- High school course catalog revisions for the 2027-28 school year are set for review

## Items for decision

The committee is scheduled to review items that will be presented to the full Board of Education for approval:

- **2026-2027 School District Scorecard** – identifies growth targets for key performance indicators across five strategic plan goals (board action requested at October 19 meeting)
- **2026-2027 School Scorecards** – individual school improvement goals and strategies aligned with district scorecard (approval at October 19 meeting)
- **High School Course Catalog revisions** for 2027-28 school year (approval at November meeting)
- **School Improvement Day Waiver Request** – to modify school code to allow three full school improvement planning days instead of six half days, covering 2027-2028 through 2031-2032 (public hearing and approval at October 19 meeting)

## Other business

- Review of norms for collaboration
- Federal grants overview for 2026-27 school year
- Concussion Oversight Team
- Academic contracts
- Next meeting scheduled for November 4
