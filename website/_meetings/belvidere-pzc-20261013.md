## Agenda preview

## The lede

- The Planning & Zoning Commission will consider a special use permit for a daycare center and indoor entertainment facility at 390 W. Chrysler Drive, proposed by Foroud Sharegh on behalf of NewRaj Investments LLC.
- The commission will review a variance request from Americold Logistics at 6765 Imron Drive to widen a new driveway entrance to 95 feet at Newburg Road, far exceeding the normal 45-foot maximum.
- Staff is recommending approval for both cases, with conditions requiring safety fencing and bollards for the daycare outdoor play area.

## Discussion items

**Case 2026-21: Daycare center at 390 W. Chrysler Drive**

The applicant plans to operate a before- and after-school program for approximately 40 school-age children in a 13,000-square-foot space within an existing multi-tenant commercial building. The facility would also include an 8,000-square-foot indoor entertainment center available to both members and the public. An outdoor play area would be located at the rear of the building, bordered by an agricultural field. Staff recommends requiring a 10-foot-high fence and concrete bollards at least 4 feet high and 12 inches wide around the play area to prevent items from leaving the area and protect against vehicle traffic.

**Case 2026-22: Driveway variance at 6765 Imron Drive**

Americold Logistics is requesting permission to build a 95-foot-wide driveway entrance on Newburg Road—including dual 30-foot curved radii on either side of a 35-foot approach—to accommodate semi-truck turning movements. The ordinance normally allows 35 feet with up to 5 feet of flare on each side for a 45-foot maximum. The wider entrance is intended to separate truck traffic from passenger vehicle traffic, prevent trucks from needing multi-point turns or encroaching into opposing lanes, and improve overall safety as the facility undergoes upgrades and expansion of its loading dock area.
