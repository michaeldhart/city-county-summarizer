## Discussion items

**Police staffing levels**: Currently down 3 officers from full staffing. Fully staffed would provide 6-7 officers per 12-hour shift; current minimum is 4 officers on patrol. Officer training updates: Two officers in Step #1 of Field Training Officer (FTO) program, one in final Step #4, one recently released to civil patrol, and one in Week #4 of Sauk Valley Police Academy.

**Fire Department eligibility list process**: State statute requires new 2-year eligibility list every two years; current list expires August 2026. Process involves 11 steps including application, written test, and prescreening interview. National Testing Network allows online testing throughout June 2026 (June 17-26, 2026 mentioned), replacing previous single-day Saturday testing format to improve candidate accessibility. Requirements: age 21-35, legal work authorization. Preference points given for paramedic certification, experience, Boone County residency, and department internship completion.

**Fire officer promotional process**: Three-year promotional cycle due February 2027. Selection criteria: 10% seniority points, 10% chief points, 40% assessment center, 40% written test. Estimated 18-21 potential candidates (21 total firefighters, minus those without required 5 years service). Assessment center includes tactical command scenario, "in basket" exercises, and simulated training presentations. I Chiefs selected over RMA Resource Management Associates due to larger pool of certified assessors, nonprofit status, and positive recommendations from other departments (e.g., Cherry Valley). Process expected to span 3-4 days total (1 day written test, 2-3 days assessment center).
