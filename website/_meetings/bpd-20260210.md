## TL;DR
- Board approved agreements for after-school services with School District 100 and law enforcement services with the City of Belvidere for the coming year.
- Approved purchase of two 2023 Ford Rangers (up to $67,000) for fleet and fire panel upgrade at Administration Office (up to $33,620).
- Executive Director received a 4.5% performance increase to $127,111.82 annually, retroactive to January 1, 2026.
- Board approved farm lease agreement for Belvidere Park Farm for $3,825 (March-November 2026).

## Decisions & votes
**All motions passed unanimously with all five commissioners present:**

- Approved meeting agenda and January 13, 2026 minutes
- Approved expense report from January 9 to February 5, 2026
- **Resolution 02-10-2026A**: Authorized agreement with Belvidere Community Unit School District No. 100 for after-school services for school year 2026-2027
- **Resolution 02-10-2026B**: Authorized Intergovernmental Agreement with City of Belvidere for law enforcement services
- Approved purchase of two 2023 Ford Rangers from Hamblock Ford (not to exceed $67,000)
- Approved fire panel upgrade at Administration Building using Pro Com Systems (not to exceed $33,620 for analog to digital conversion)
- Approved farm lease with Grant Anderson for Farm #2403 Tract 7767 for $3,825 (March 1-November 30, 2026, with 50% payments due May 1 and December 1)
- Approved 4.5% performance increase for Executive Director ($5,223.82 annual increase to $127,111.82 total, retroactive to January 1, 2026)
- Approved but did not release closed session minutes dated August 12, 2025
