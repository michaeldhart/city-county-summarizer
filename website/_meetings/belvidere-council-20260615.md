## TL;DR

- Approved approximately $3 million in expenditures ($1.76M General/Special Fund; $1.26M Water/Sewer Fund)
- Passed ordinance transferring Santa Claus House to Belvidere Township Park District for permanent display at Dottie Park
- Approved equipment purchases including walk-behind saw ($21,132), Bobcat Toolcat work machine ($80,195), and SCBA fill station/storage system ($23,145)
- Accepted $20,180 grant from Illinois Office of State Fire Marshal for fire equipment
- Placed four special use permit ordinances on file for first reading (oversized garage, outdoor storage, commercial entertainment, mural)

## Decisions & votes

**Expenditures approved (10-0 on each):**
- General & Special Fund: $1,756,804.92
- Water & Sewer Fund: $1,260,065.15

**Ord. #766H (10-0, with Brereton voting nay per minutes but transcript indicates 10 in favor):** Authorized intergovernmental agreement transferring Santa Claus House structure to Belvidere Township Park District. Note: Scrivener's error identified - "Santa Clause" should read "Santa Claus."

**Equipment purchases (all 10-0):**
- Husqvarna walk-behind saw from Esch Construction Supply: $21,132.12 (Water Depreciation Account)
- 2026 Bobcat UWS6 Toolcat from Bobcat of Rockford: $80,194.84 (split between Depreciation $79,355.04 and Sewer Expense $839.80)
- MAKO SCBA fill station and cascade storage from Air One Equipment: $23,145 ($20,180 from FY26 Small Equipment Grant; $2,965 from capital funds)

**Res. #2026-18 (10-0):** Directed Planning Department to publish annual City of Belvidere Zoning Map (note: numbered 2026-17 on resolution but 2026-18 on agenda)

**Minutes approved unanimously:** June 1, 2026 regular meeting; June 8, 2026 Committee of the Whole

## Discussion items

**Santa Claus House transfer:** Originally an Eagle Scout project by Charlie Beckel, structure currently housed in Leith building but difficult to move (required staying until after 1:00 a.m. during last transport). Park District will pour permanent slab at Dottie Park; structure already wired with breaker panel. Will enhance Christmas walk programming.

**Fire equipment grant:** State Fire Marshal grant for $20,180 was unexpected—city did not initially think it would receive award.

**Zoning map updates:** Annual publication required by state. Changes include boundary line cleanups near St. James Cemetery and Walmart area, plus recent annexations.

**Block party/street closure request:** Alderman Peterson inquired; item scheduled for next council meeting for final approval.

**Executive session:** Scheduled executive session for collective bargaining matters cancelled due to absent parties; will reschedule when all participants available.

## Financial / operational notes

**Total appropriations approved:** $3,016,870.07 across all funds

**Equipment procurement:** Husqvarna saw was low bid with all bidders quoting same specifications.

**Grant leverage:** Fire department successfully leveraged $20,180 state grant to cover 87% of $23,145 SCBA equipment cost, with only $2,965 local match required.

**Water/Sewer capital:** Major equipment purchases funded from depreciation accounts rather than operating budgets.
