## Agenda preview

- The committee is set to review the district's strategic plan progress, specifically Goal 5 on operational resources
- A first reading of the 2026 tax levy is scheduled, along with a sales tax update
- The committee will consider a request to modify state school code to allow driver's education fees up to $400, which would require a public hearing
- Three Sunday facility use requests are on the agenda: a high school dance in the gymnasium, a wrestling tournament, and a performing arts center rental for the Rockford Symphony Youth Orchestra

## Items for decision

- **Sunday facility use requests:** Three requests for weekend building access—a Belvidere High School dance in the gymnasium, a Belvidere Bandits wrestling tournament, and a performing arts center rental for the Rockford Symphony Youth Orchestra on November 22, 2026
- **Excess property disposal:** Disposal of broken batteries, calculators, and charging bases from Belvidere High School
- **Driver's education fee increase:** Request to modify Illinois School Code to allow the district to charge up to $400 for driver's education courses (currently capped at $250 without a public hearing), with a recommendation to hold a public hearing and approve the request at the November 16 school board meeting

## Other business

- Strategic plan update focusing on Goal 5: Operational Resources
- Sales tax revenue update and history
- First reading of the 2026 tax levy with preliminary levy worksheets
- Facility master plan school meetings update
