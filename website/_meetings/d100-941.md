## Discussion items (no vote)

**Strategic Plan Goal 4 - Premier Workforce**: Administration provided monthly progress review on the district's strategic plan, specifically Goal 4. Presentation included the district's scorecard and schools' scorecard.

**Section 6 Policy Review**: Committee reviewed 15 policies related to instruction, including School Year Calendar and Day (6:20), Curriculum Development (6:40), School Wellness (6:50 - requested by Finance Department for audit purposes), Program for the Gifted (6:130), Home and Hospital Instruction (6:150), English Learners (6:160), Extracurricular and Co-Curricular Activities (6:190), Access to Electronic Networks (6:235), Field Trips (6:240), Assemblies and Ceremonies (6:255), Grading and Promotions (6:280), Homework (6:290), Graduation Requirements (6:300), and High School Credit for Students in Grade 7 or 8 (6:315).

**Additional Policy Reviews**: Reviewed five policies from other sections: Personal Technology and Social Media (5:125 - updated to replace NON-PRESS Policy 5:65 Cell Phone Usage, which will be retired), Solicitations By or From Staff (5:140), Return to Work Program (5:181), Sick Days, Vacation, Holidays, and Leave (5:330), and Student Behavior (7:190).

**BEST Memorandum of Agreement**: Reviewed draft MOA between the Board of Education and Belvidere Educational Support Team regarding educational leave (existing agreement).

**Homeschool Requirements for District Sports**: Discussion of requirements for homeschooled students to participate in District 100 athletic programs (discussion item only).
