## Discussion items (no vote)

**Norms for Collaboration**: Committee reviewed established collaboration norms including starting/ending on time, reviewing materials in advance, respecting time limits, assuming positive intent, and sharing discussion time. These norms have been in use since the 2022-23 school year.

**2024-2025 School District Scorecard**: Administration presented annual scorecard identifying growth targets for Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) across five strategic plan goals. Scorecard tracks progress throughout the year. School scorecards scheduled for review at October Education Services Committee with board action requested at October School Board meeting.

**Special Education Department Updates**: Committee received updates from the Special Education Department.

**Summer School Programming for 2026**: Administration presented summer school programming plans. No action required at this time.

**Strategic Plan Mid-Year Summary**: Administration provided mid-year update on Strategic Plan implementation, highlighting student achievements and staff efforts during the first half of the academic year. Report demonstrates continued commitment to five core goal areas.

**Mathematics Curriculum Resources Adoption and Extension**: Committee discussed mathematics curriculum resources adoption and extension.

**World Language Curriculum Resource Adoption**: Committee reviewed world language curriculum resource adoption proposals.
