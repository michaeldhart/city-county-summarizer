## The lede

- Committee reviewed Strategic Plan Goal 5 (Operational Resources) progress and district scorecard updates
- Discussed FY27 tentative budget updates and budget efficiency parameters, including energy conservation management guidelines
- Reviewed multiple procurement items: Microsoft renewal, annual fuel bid, copy paper bid for FY27
- Considered disposal of excess district property and Sunday facility use request for soccer games

## Attendance & housekeeping

**Members of record:** Jorge Herrera, Holly Houk, Brian Hoemke

**Location:** Lincoln Elementary School - 1011 Bonus Ave., Belvidere

**Next meetings:** October 6 at Everest High School; November 3 at Meehan Elementary School; December 1 at Belvidere Central Middle School

## Decisions & votes

_No content._

## Discussion items (no vote)

- **Strategic Plan Update - Goal 5:** Administration provided monthly review of district progress on Goal 5 (Operational Resources), including district scorecard and schools' scorecard
- **Budget Efficiencies and Parameters:** Reviewed budget efficiency measures and Cenergistic Energy Conservation Management guidelines and agreement
- **FY27 Tentative Budget Update:** Discussed updates to the FY27 budget proposal
- **2026 Microsoft Renewal:** Reviewed Microsoft software renewal proposal from CDW
- **Annual Fuel Bid:** Reviewed fuel bid summary and Petroleum Traders Corporation proposal for 2026-27
- **FY27 Copy Paper Bid:** Reviewed copy paper bid summary for fiscal year 2027
- **Excess Property Disposal:** Considered disposal of three categories of excess property: miscellaneous textbooks from Lincoln Elementary School, two digital pianos and benches from Belvidere North High School, and old radios, batteries and charger bases district-wide
- **Sunday Facility Use:** Reviewed Riiize FC Soccer Games request for use of CMS Practice Fields 1 & 2 on Sundays
