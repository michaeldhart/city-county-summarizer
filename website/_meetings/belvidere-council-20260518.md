## The lede
- Council approved a 6.5% sewer rate increase (Ord. #764H) and nearly $2.8M in expenditures split between General and Water/Sewer funds.
- Council passed Resolution 2026-16 opposing the Illinois BUILD Act, which would strip local zoning and land-use authority from municipalities.
- Craig Wilcox appointed Director of Buildings at $80,000 salary (down from $89,000 prior incumbent) with 8 weeks vacation; amendment to reduce to 4 weeks failed 2-8.
- Four commission appointments approved: two to Planning & Zoning (Druckrey, Hyland) and two to Historic Preservation (Coniglio, Dobberfuhl).
- Council approved purchase of two John Deere excavators totaling ~$486K from capital funds.

## Decisions & votes

**Ord. #764H (Sewer rate increase):** Passed 9-1 (Brereton opposed). Amends appendix A to modify sewer rates effective May 1, 2026.

**Res. #2026-16 (Opposing BUILD Act):** Passed 10-0. Formally opposes state legislation that would preempt local zoning, planning, and land-use authority.

**Commission appointments:** All passed 10-0:
- Daniel Druckrey (P&Z, 5-year term)
- Arthur Hyland (P&Z, 5-year term)
- Kimberly Coniglio (Historic Preservation, 3-year term)
- Sonya Dobberfuhl (Historic Preservation, 3-year term)

**Craig Wilcox appointment as Director of Buildings:** Passed 8-2 (Gramkowski, Brereton opposed). $80,000 salary, 8 weeks paid vacation. Amendment by Brereton to reduce vacation to 4 weeks failed 2-8 (Gramkowski, Brereton in favor).

**Hampton Lenzini & Renwick grant application ($3,600):** Passed 10-0. FRA Railroad Crossing Elimination Program for Appleton Road Grade Separation.

**Testing Service Corporation contract ($22,750):** Passed 9-1 (Brereton opposed). Material testing for Parking Lot #7 expansion.

**John Deere 190G FT4 excavator ($345,532.85):** Passed 10-0. Includes trade-in of 1999 Komatsu excavator.

**John Deere 60P mini excavator ($140,446.49):** Passed 10-0. Old mini excavator will be auctioned or retained by street department.

**Expenditures approved:**
- General & Special Funds: $1,761,694.95 (passed 10-0)
- Water & Sewer Funds: $990,837.55 (passed 10-0)

## Discussion items

**BUILD Act opposition:** Lengthy discussion of Illinois legislation that would strip local control over zoning, density, lot coverage, parking requirements, and flood mitigation. Mayor Morris and Planner DelRose argued this would eliminate local discretion on development standards, potentially increase flooding, congest streets (impeding emergency response), and raise taxes citywide to fund infrastructure for "affordable" projects. No one could answer how cost savings would be guaranteed to buyers vs. pocketed by developers. Mayor questioned who is funding legislators to push the bill.

**Craig Wilcox appointment:** Debated vacation policy (8 weeks vs. standard 4 weeks after 15–16 years). Gramkowski objected on policy grounds, concerned about precedent. Mayor argued short notice (4.5 weeks) left no time to recruit externally; Wilcox is only qualified candidate in department, familiar with ongoing projects, and taking a pay cut. Peterson called amendment to reduce vacation "extremely bad faith" after hiring terms negotiated. Hoinacki supported for succession planning. Brereton maintained it sets bad precedent.

**Director of Buildings salary:** Current director paid $89,000; Wilcox will receive $80,000 ($9,000 savings). Mayor noted external hire would likely cost $115–125K and delay projects.

**Mini excavator disposition:** Assistant Director Keck confirmed old mini excavator may be retained by street department for tight spaces or auctioned.
