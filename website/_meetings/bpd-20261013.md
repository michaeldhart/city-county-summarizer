## The lede

- The Park District Board approved $12,390 for architectural services to restore the exterior of Baltic Mill, including construction documents and bidding assistance.
- Commissioners agreed to switch to a new Maintain X work order system for $8,698, covering implementation and software licenses for 13 staff members, citing better features and easier scheduling than the current system.
- The board renewed a $250,000 certificate of deposit with Midland States Bank for 12 months at 3.65% interest.
- Staff provided an update on a sports market and feasibility study, with discussion focused on comparing a potential new field house versus upgrading Prairie Fields, including year-round versus seasonal use and artificial turf versus grass.

## Decisions & votes

**Approved unanimously by voice vote:**
- Meeting agenda for October 13, 2026
- General meeting minutes from September 8, 2026

**Approved unanimously by roll call vote:**
- Expense report from September 4 to October 8, 2026
- Studio GWA architectural consulting contract for Baltic Mill exterior restoration, not to exceed $12,390
- Purchase of a 52-inch Scag stand-on mower from Cherry Valley Landscape for $4,465, trading in one 72-inch Exmark mower and one 36-inch walk-behind mower
- Contract with Maintain X work order system for $8,698, covering implementation and licenses for 13 staff
- Renewal of $250,000 certificate of deposit with Midland States Bank for 12 months at 3.65% annual interest, effective October 23, 2026
- Adjournment at 6:11 p.m.

## Discussion items

**Sports market and feasibility study:** The board reviewed input to the study and discussed next steps. Conversation centered on comparing a potential field house with upgrading Prairie Fields, weighing year-round facility use versus seasonal field use, costs of each option, community needs and support, local interest, and the pros, cons, upkeep, preservation and costs of artificial turf versus grass fields.

**Maintain X work order system:** Staff explained concerns with the current program and benefits of the new system, including improved maintenance scheduling, more features, and easier preventive maintenance and work order scheduling. The board compared costs between the current and new systems.

**Pickleball court striping:** Discussion occurred during the Parks & Recreation report.

**Baltic Mill project scope:** Commissioners discussed the scope of the exterior restoration project.

## Financial / operational notes

The board reviewed the September 2026 income statement without discussion. Administrative Services reported on the tax distribution schedule and mentioned an investment meeting. The 2026 tax levy for fiscal year 2027 included a levy revenue review. Staff provided a fiscal year 2027 budget review and public hearing schedule. The district received an update on the general obligation bond issuance process and timetable. Executive Director Jen Jacky reported on e-bikes and enterprise zone matters. Parks & Recreation staff updated the board on an ADA compliance project, the Santa House at Doty Park, and ice rink lighting. Recreation staff reported on the winter/spring program guide. A revised 2027 board meeting schedule was presented.
