## Agenda preview

## The lede

- The Belvidere Township Park District Board of Commissioners is scheduled to meet October 13, 2026 at Baltic Mill to review consulting services for exterior restoration of the Baltic Mill building.
- The board will consider trading in a mower with Cherry Valley Landscape and switching to a new work order system called Maintain X.
- Commissioners are expected to receive updates on the 2026 tax levy for fiscal year 2027, the fiscal year 2027 budget, and the process and timeline for issuing general obligation bonds.
