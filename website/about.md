# Backgrounders

A backgrounder is the reference material a reporter keeps beside them on a
beat: how a government is organized, who currently serves, and when its
bodies meet. There is one here for each government on the wire.

- [Boone County]({{ '/about/boone-county/' | relative_url }}) - covers the county board, its committees and appointed bodies, and the two independent conservation districts.
- [City of Belvidere]({{ '/about/belvidere/' | relative_url }}) - covers the City Council, the Committee of the Whole, and the city's boards and commissions.
- [Belvidere Community Unit School District 100]({{ '/about/d100/' | relative_url }}) - covers the Board of Education and its standing committees.
- [Belvidere Township Park District]({{ '/about/bpd/' | relative_url }}) - covers the elected Board of Commissioners.

These are compiled from each government's own published sources and carry
the date they were last rebuilt. For how the dispatches themselves are
written, see [How this is written]({{ '/method/' | relative_url }}).
