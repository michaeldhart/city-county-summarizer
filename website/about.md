## Overview

Boone County is a county in northern Illinois with a population of 53,448 (2020 census). The county seat is Belvidere, located at 1212 Logan Avenue. The county encompasses 282.039 square miles (280.741 square miles land, 1.298 square miles water) and is included in the Rockford, Illinois metropolitan area.

Boone County government operates under Illinois statute with a 12-member elected County Board as its primary legislative body. The Board uses a Committee of the Whole (COTW) structure for deliberation and policy development. Two standing committees—Administrative & Legislative and Finance, Taxation & Salaries—handle the bulk of the Board's work. The Board appoints members to a range of specialized commissions covering planning, zoning, health, conservation, and emergency management. County operations are carried out by both elected constitutional officers (Clerk, Sheriff, State's Attorney, Coroner, Treasurer, Circuit Clerk) and appointed department heads.

The county was formed on March 3, 1836, out of Winnebago County and named for Kentucky frontiersman Daniel Boone. It is divided into nine townships and includes the cities of Belvidere and Loves Park (partial), plus several villages.

## The County Board

- **Composition:** 12 elected members, four from each of three districts.
- **Terms:** Members serve either two or four-year terms; terms expire in December of even-numbered years.
- **Chair and Vice Chair:** Elected by the members of the Board (not directly by voters). The County Board Members page identifies Rodney Riley as Vice-Chairman; no Chair is named in the sources provided.
- **Meeting schedule:** County Board meetings are held at 6:30 PM in the County Board Room at 1212 Logan Avenue. (Specific day/frequency not stated in sources; see BoardDocs for current schedule.)
- **Committee of the Whole model:** Boone County uses the Committee of the Whole (COTW) structure, in which the entire legislative body sits as a committee for discussion and debate. All Board members participate in the two standing COTWs. This model is used "for the purposes of discussion and debate on a variety of issues" before formal Board action.

## Departments

Boone County maintains the following departments and offices. Where a specific officeholder or head is identified in the sources, they are noted:

- **17th Judicial Circuit Court:** Comprises Boone and Winnebago counties; hears all case types. Boone County is in the third subcircuit.
- **Administration:** Handles budget, finance, human resources, purchasing, IT, and interdepartmental coordination.
- **Animal Services:** Government-owned animal control, funded by vaccination registration fees.
- **Assessment Office:** Oversees property assessment, exemptions, and ownership records.
- **Building & Zoning:** Enforces building and zoning codes, issues permits, conducts inspections.
- **Clerk & Recorder:** Amy E. Ohlsen, Boone County Clerk & Recorder. Processes and records legal documents, birth/death certificates, conducts elections, computes property taxes, maintains land transaction records (since 1838) and vital records (since 1877).
  - Staff: Giselle Lenover (Chief Deputy Recorder), Laura Bettis (Chief Deputy Clerk), Carin Hunt, Jessica Holmes, Carianne Anderson, Tami Decker (Deputy Clerks).
  - Office: 1212 Logan Avenue, Suite 103, Belvidere, IL 61008. Hours: Monday–Friday 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM.
- **Clerk of the Circuit Court:** Maintains court case records, collects/disburses fines and fees, summons jurors.
- **Coroner:** Law enforcement agency performing death investigations per Illinois statute.
- **Corrections & County Jail:** Under Sheriff's authority; houses individuals awaiting trial or serving sentences up to one year.
- **Emergency Management:** Plans, trains, and coordinates disaster response and mitigation.
- **Geographic Information Systems & Maps:** Maintains county-wide GIS databases and property mapping.
- **Health Department:** Public health services, clinics, environmental health. Governed by the Board of Health.
- **Highway Department:** Inspects and maintains county highways and bridges, issues permits, designs projects, assists townships.
- **Planning Department:** Administers comprehensive plan, zoning code, subdivision code; processes special uses, rezonings, variances, text amendments; provides staff support to Planning Commission and Zoning Board of Appeals.
- **Probation:** Operates under 17th Judicial Circuit Chief Judge; administers community supervision for offenders.
- **Public Defender:** Represents indigent criminal defendants and parents in abuse/neglect cases.
- **Sheriff's Office:** Enforces federal, state, and county laws in unincorporated areas; fundamental duty to protect lives and property.
- **State's Attorney:** Prosecutes criminal violations of Illinois statutes occurring in Boone County (except Belvidere traffic offenses, which are prosecuted locally).
- **Storm Water and Pollution Control:** Manages storm water pollution controls for the Belvidere-Boone County area.
- **Treasurer:** Receives and deposits county revenues, mails and collects real estate tax bills, distributes funds to taxing districts, collects the 1% food and beverage tax supporting the Community Building Complex.
