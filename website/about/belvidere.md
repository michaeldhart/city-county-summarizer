## Overview

Belvidere is the county seat of Boone County, Illinois, located in the north-central part of the state along the Kishwaukee River. The city is approximately 75 miles northwest of downtown Chicago and 12 miles east of Rockford. With a 2020 Census population of 25,339, Belvidere is an industrial community surrounded by rich farmland, known as the "City of Murals" for its public art installations throughout its historic downtown districts. The city encompasses 12.38 square miles (12.14 square miles of land).

Belvidere operates under a mayor-council form of government with an aldermanic city council. The city is governed by a mayor, city clerk, and treasurer, along with ten aldermen—two elected from each of the city's five wards. The city's government oversees municipal operations including police, fire, public works, water and sewer services, community development, and various appointed boards and commissions.

## Form of government

- **Type**: Mayor-council, aldermanic system
- **Wards**: Five wards, with two aldermen elected from each ward
- **Terms**: Four-year terms for mayor, city clerk, treasurer, and all aldermen
- **Staggering**: Aldermanic seats within each ward are staggered so that one seat from each ward is elected every two years

## The City Council

**Composition**: The City Council consists of 10 aldermen (two from each of five wards) plus the mayor, who presides.

**Meeting schedule**: 
- Regular City Council meetings are held the 1st and 3rd Monday of each month at 6:00 PM in the City Council Chambers at City Hall (401 Whitney Blvd.)
- When a meeting falls on a legal holiday, it is held the next business day at the same time
- Meetings are streamed to YouTube

**Committee of the Whole**: The full council meets as a Committee of the Whole twice monthly to review matters before formal action:
- **2nd Monday** of each month at 6:00 PM: Building, Zoning and Planning; Public Works
- **4th Monday** of each month at 6:00 PM: Public Safety; Finance and Personnel

**Important note on Committee of the Whole records**: The Committee of the Whole publishes agendas and packets but **does not publish minutes**. The video recording (streamed to YouTube) is the only record of what was actually discussed.

## Boards and commissions

**Planning & Zoning Commission**: Reviews planning and land use matters. Meets the 2nd Tuesday of each month at 6:00 PM in the City Council Chambers. Publishes agendas. **Note**: This commission also sits as the Zoning Board of Appeals when hearing variance and special use requests, so there is no separate ZBA.

**Historic Preservation Commission**: Reviews matters related to historic preservation and locally landmarked properties. Meets the 4th Tuesday of each month at 5:30 PM in the City Council Chambers. Publishes agendas.

**Board of Fire & Police Commissioners**: Handles civil service matters, testing, and hiring for police and fire departments. Meets irregularly. Publishes agendas when meetings are scheduled.

**Bodies that exist but publish no agendas or minutes**: The following boards and commissions are appointed by the mayor and operate as separate entities or with limited public documentation:
- Firefighters' Pension Board
- Police Pension Board  
- Library Board (governing Ida Public Library, a separate legal entity)
- Boone County Council on Aging Board (the city appoints some members; separate legal entity)
- Community Building Complex of Boone County Board (the city appoints some members; separate legal entity)
- Fire Department #2 Foreign Fire Insurance Fund

**Note**: There is no separate Liquor Commission; liquor licensing is handled administratively by the City Clerk. Economic development is handled by Growth Dimensions, a separate organization, not a city board.
