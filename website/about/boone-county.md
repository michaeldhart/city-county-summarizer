## Overview

Boone County is located in northern Illinois, with its county seat in Belvidere. According to the U.S. Census Bureau, the county covers 282.039 square miles (280.741 square miles land, 1.298 square miles water) and had a population of 53,448 as of the 2020 census. The county is part of the Rockford, Illinois metropolitan area and is bordered by Rock and Walworth counties in Wisconsin to the north, McHenry County to the east, DeKalb County to the south, and Winnebago County to the west. Boone County was formed on March 3, 1836, out of Winnebago County and named for Kentucky frontiersman Daniel Boone.

The county government operates under Illinois statute with a 12-member elected County Board as its legislative body. The Board uses a Committee of the Whole (COTW) structure for much of its deliberative work. The county provides services through a mix of elected constitutional officers (Sheriff, Clerk & Recorder, Circuit Clerk, Coroner, Treasurer, State's Attorney) and appointed department heads. Two separately governed conservation districts—the Boone County Conservation District and the Boone County Soil & Water Conservation District—operate independently with their own elected boards.

### The Committee of the Whole model

Boone County uses a Committee of the Whole (COTW) structure in which the entire legislative body sits as one large committee for discussion and debate. All members of the County Board are members of each COTW. The county has two standing COTWs; their meeting schedules are **not published** on the website.

## Departments

The county operates through the following departments. Elected officials are noted; appointed department heads are **not published** on the website.

- **17th Judicial Circuit Court** – Shared with Winnebago County; hears all case types
- **Administration** – Coordinates budget, finance, human resources, purchasing, IT
- **Animal Services** – State-certified animal control; funded through vaccination registration
- **Assessment Office** – Oversees property assessments, exemptions, and ownership records
- **Building & Zoning** – Enforces building and zoning codes; issues permits and conducts inspections
- **Clerk & Recorder** – *Amy E. Ohlsen, elected Clerk & Recorder*; processes legal documents, birth/death/marriage certificates; conducts elections; maintains land records
- **Clerk of the Circuit Court** – Maintains court case records; collects fines and fees; summons jurors
- **Coroner** – *Name not published*; investigates deaths per statute; law enforcement agency
- **Corrections & County Jail** – Under authority of the Sheriff; custody and sentencing facility
- **Emergency Management** – Planning, training, mitigation, and disaster response coordination
- **Geographic Information Systems & Maps** – Maintains county maps and GIS databases; property data website
- **Health Department** – Public health services, clinics, environmental health
- **Highway Department** – Inspects and maintains county highways and bridges; issues permits; designs projects
- **Planning Department** – Administers comprehensive plan, zoning code, and subdivision code; processes special uses, rezonings, variances
- **Probation** – Operates under the Chief Judge; administers community supervision for offenders
- **Public Defender** – *Name not published*; represents indigent criminal defendants and parties in certain juvenile/abuse-neglect cases
- **Sheriff's Office** – *Name not published, elected Sheriff*; law enforcement in unincorporated areas; constitutional and statutory duties
- **State's Attorney** – *Name not published, elected State's Attorney*; prosecutes violations of Illinois criminal statutes
- **Storm Water and Pollution Control** – Addresses storm water pollution controls for Belvidere-Boone County
- **Treasurer** – *Name not published, elected Treasurer*; receives and deposits county revenues; mails and collects property tax; collects 1% food and beverage tax
