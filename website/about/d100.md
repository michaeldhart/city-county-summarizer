## Overview

Belvidere Community Unit School District 100 (BCUSD 100) is an independently governed public school district spanning portions of Boone, McHenry, and DeKalb counties in Illinois. The district's central office is located at 1201 5th Avenue, Belvidere, IL 61008.

A "unit" school district in Illinois provides education for all grades from kindergarten through high school within its boundaries, governed by an independently elected board of education. BCUSD 100 is governed by a seven-member Board of Education that serves as the primary policy-making body, with day-to-day management delegated to the Superintendent and administrative staff. The district publishes agendas, minutes, and meeting materials through the Diligent Community platform at district100.community.highbond.com.

## The Board of Education

- **Member count:** 7 members
- **Election structure:** Four members are elected from Belvidere Township, three from the remaining townships served by the district
- **Terms:** Four-year terms
- **Compensation:** Non-paid positions
- **Election type:** Nonpartisan; candidates do not run under political party affiliations
- **Meeting schedule:** Regular meetings held on the third Monday of the month at 6:00 PM; Special meetings held on the first Thursday of the month at 5:30 PM (unless otherwise noted on agenda)
- **Location:** Central Office Board Room (lower level), 1201 5th Avenue, Belvidere, IL 61008
- **Additional meetings:** Workshops, retreats, town halls, hearings, and special meetings are held periodically and filed as separate meeting types but are treated as Board of Education meetings
